Dear Zindagi |Movie Review

Iqra Malik

self-discovery in Dear Zindagi-Movie

In almost every culture religion, nationality, and region people have self-discovery issues. They all go from self-identity crisis, they all make efforts for inner peace.

They all first motivate their selves after something else, and they just know their worth and after that, they discover their selves.

In Gauri Shinde’s “Dear Zindagi” the character “Kaira”, who is a cinematographer, faces problems in her relationships with her parents, with guys. She never allowed anyone for the marriage, she left Raghu, Sid, and Rumi because she had a fear that they will leave her.

This fear to feel safe you have to be fearless, fear of losing someone (Thantophobia), and fear of one can leave us. She rejected many guys and goes through mental trauma and insomnia.

After that, she meets a therapist Jug(Dr: Jahangir)who taught her how to live with a free mind. When new patterns or new hobbies seem to become in life then think about it consciously Genius is about knowing when to stop. Just make yourself free and think about what things you have to do to make your relationships stronger, how to overcome your fears.

He said” If we don’t hold our life’s steering than someone else will sit on driving seat “So we are the driver of our life he depends on us where we turn our vehicle, towards negativity (depression, stress anxiety, trauma, confusion)or to positivity (happiness, inner peace satisfaction).

This depends on us, how we can discover ourselves. And one thing to feel happy and satisfying you have to live in the present, don’t blame yourself for any failure because it is one step closer to success.

Hellen Keller said: Don’t think of today's failure but of the success that may come tomorrow.

You have to make your inner strength to bear all the failure because you are getting experience of success, that which things are a hurdle in success, so you can remove it. Jug said: Don’t let your past to blackmail your present, to ruin a beautiful future.

Just know your worth and do yourself what you want to do, just blow a flame of self-discovery in your heart and discover yourself what things that are ruining your inner peace just kick than out” We all are our teachers in the school of life.

Hurdles and Obstacles are part of success, or we can say we have to go through all the suffering because we can’t get even a single bread without moving from our place, so it is a basic thing, hardships will come, and we have to face all these intensely.

In Dear Zindagi we have seen our protagonist Kaira has motivated herself when she was suffering from her breakup and after she motivated herself to talk to her parents about her childhood miseries which let her discover herself and the relationship was sorted out between them.

Ending

In the closure, we know that people may suffer from an identity crisis but what is the major thing to consider is to know yourself as Kaira discovered herself.
