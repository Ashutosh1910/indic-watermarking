Week 247: The Prep

Aaron Charles

Sarah and I are both list makers. We like to plan and then feel the rush of crossing an item off the to-do list. But this week…I’m sorry…but my wife took things a little too far.

The to-do list she made could have been mistaken for War and Peace. A giraffe could have used it as a scarf. My point is the list was really long.

We planned out everything we had to do that weekend. Clean the table? Check. Clean up the entryway? Check. Organize our shoes? Check. Turn the turkey? Check.

Wait…what was that last one?

Well, you see, this year COVID-19 changed our Thanksgiving plans. Usually we have big family get togethers, but we decided to forego those this year. So Sarah and I would be making the Thanksgiving meal ourselves for the first time. You might remember that we did a mini-version a few years back, but that was different.

This year, we’d be making a full meal with all the fixin’s. So, naturally, we had to do some prep work around the house.

Not only did that involve cleaning up inside, but the biggest job was out in the yard. On Saturday, we spent a couple hours raking and blowing the leaves in our yard into piles for the city leaf pickup. I must tell you — this was not Sarah’s favorite job. Usually her love for manning the leaf blower covers over the frustrations of all the leaves caught up inside our fenced-in front yard. But we had A LOT of leaves. Still, we both stuck it out and got the job done. Item checked off the list.

We flew through that list. On Sunday, we took a look at it again and there were only a few unchecked boxes. We had done the laundry, cleaned the house, and all those leaves were neatly clustered into piles outside.

There was no cooking on this Sunday, but that’s because we were prepping for the big meal — THE meal. There would be enough cooking to come. This weekend was about prep work. That included setting the table, and — as you can see from the pictures on this post — Sarah outdid herself.

The thing about preparation is that it takes foresight. It takes looking ahead and planning a bit. I’m not always great at that, but Sarah took the initiative and I came along for the ride. This weekend felt so healthy, because we were putting in the work now so that we’d have it easier later on. We were working together. We were laying the foundation for the coming week.

Crossing items off the to-do list always feels good. But crossing items off the to-do list with your best friend, your partner, the love of your life? That’s even better. That’s the stuff.

As you know, Sarah’s the go-getter here. She puts her mind to things and gets the done. I’m just happy to come along for the ride. And — if I’m being honest — I learn a lot from my wife. Every single day, I learn so much. About hard work. About planning. About doing things the right way.

I’m a lucky guy, that’s for sure.
