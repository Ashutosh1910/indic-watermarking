Sober Summary

The Princess Diaries earns its title as a classic effortlessly. It is a fresh twist on the coming of age/young-teen movie by still managing to maintain all the tropes we’ve come to know and love. The film is fun and doesn’t take itself too seriously (but not to the point where it’s stupid) and has a lot of common scenes you can find in a lot of other films of its type but that doesn’t take away from the fact that it’s still a great watch. If you haven’t seen it yet because you absolutely do not watch movies despite how much you love them (me), then I highly recommend you give this one a chance.
