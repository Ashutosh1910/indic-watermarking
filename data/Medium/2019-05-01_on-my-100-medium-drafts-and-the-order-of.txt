On My 100 Medium Drafts and the Order of My 100 Medium Drafts

Sarah Scullin

I currently have 100 drafts in Medium. Not published articles (those are in their 30 somethings right now, vaping and wondering if they should have kids). But unfinished unpublished drafts from the past two years since the last time I cleaned out my drafts folder? I have 100.

Now, I’m not bragging about the fact I have 100 drafts in Medium. I cannot stress that enough. It’s really important to me that if people are going to hate me, they hate me for an accurate beef and not something I didn’t do, like brag on myself for having a certain number of drafts on Medium.

So, fair beefs include: my tendency to interrupt, talking about work too much, not knowing the right place to start telling a story, my fantastic lipstick game, inadequately masking my intense hatred of your significant other, my need to be right all the time, the fact that I am actually usually right most of the time, unsolicited parenting advice, oversharing about bodily functions, and the fact that a combination of anxiety and laziness induces me to only do the things that come easily, thereby giving the false impression that I am not a hot mess and that I have my shit together when my shit is actually quite diffuse.

Unacceptable beef: she bragged about the number of unfinished drafts she has on Medium. If anything, these drafts give me anxiety as they are a record of unfinished, never to be finished, unfinishable, business. You want proof it’s not a brag? Here’s the entirety of one of the drafts:

Is this a joke joke or just a joke like the way my “skin care regimen” is a joke? No, I’m not fucking “ready to publish,” Medium, jesus.

I don’t have my shit together enough to keep track of actual pen and paper for jotting down ideas, so I do the 10th best thing and start an ENTIRE NEW DRAFT in Medium, where it’s impossible to organize the drafts or search through the ideas, or delete anything without being asked if “I’m sure” and a warning that anything I delete will be gone forever.
