Friendship

Brett Beitlich

What is friendship and how is it defined? There are actions and attributes that each of us look for in those we us the reserved “friend” title for. My own characteristics include someone who looks out for me like I would look out for them. What that equates to is understanding friendship is not just something which happens when times are good, I need someone around when I am reeling away in my own mind and someone there to help me redirect myself towards positivity. Another characteristic is kindness, simply put I need to be around those in which act in the directions of constant kindness. This means if there is an elderly person needing assistance across the street, I know my current friends would gladly help that person out. The last characteristic is consistency, that does not mean I need some even keeled person who shows no emotion, far from it. I think of it more in the nature of my friends know themselves and they are consistent with who they want to be, and they are proud of it. They are comfortable in their own skin and are exactly that when we are around one another. This characteristic is by far the hardest to come by, as so many of us currently seek the approval of others around us.

I write about this topic because of the constant barrage of negativity we find ourselves in currently in history. I have seen recently people post on social media that they cannot and will not be friends with someone who has a difference in opinion around religion, politics or careers. Are we so individualistic we cannot see past ourselves and appreciate the differences of others in the world? Are we so set in our ways, we want nothing but those around us to think, act and be what we are? I chose the opposite and I appreciate the fact my current circle will allow me to have a difference in opinion and state it in a conversation that is productive and heard.

Friends are those around you which make you become more than you are and support you during that development. It may not always be the sweetest of words whispered, but they are told and come from a heart that is supportive and critical in helping you become more than you think of yourself. Friendship is holding onto the moments which make up our own being and knowing those moments are precision enough to be shared when together. That means stories of fun times, pictures of adventures together and sharing those moments. It also means having the discretion to keep secret when a friend is struggling and not exposing those times for the benefit of a “like” on social media.

As you travel this journey of life, I encourage you to look at the current circle in which you find yourself. Are those around you contributing to your greatness or are they there to find the faults in the decisions you make and use that negativity to make themselves feel better. Find a group that loves you for you, hold on to them tight, show them how important they are to you and I am sure they will do the same in return.
