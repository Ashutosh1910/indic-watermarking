My fairytale love story

Umzila Kawulandelwa

My angel man was cremated yesterday. It still feels so surreal just thinking the love of my life is no more. Anyway today is the first day of my grieving and mourning journey. We’ve been so busy with the logistics surrounding his passing that I haven’t had time to sit with my thoughts like I am right now.

I’ve been messaging people who lost loved ones and are still here today asking them how they survived such tragic loss cos in the first few days I wanted to die. It wasn’t until my therapy session on Tuesday that I realized I actually didn’t want to die, I just wanted the pain to stop. I’m grateful to my therapist for making me realize that.

I always told my angel that my worst fear was one of us dying before we got married because black families do not acknowledge girlfriends and boyfriends. You’re either a wife, husband or nothing. My worst fear happened. He died before we were husband and wife but true to his nature, he instructed his family to take care of me.

On the day of his passing I went over to his house to see his family. Once I arrived everyone was called into the lounge and they gave me an opportunity to speak. I told them of my fear of my pain as his girlfriend not being acknowledged. They listened and held space for me. I was included in everything till the very end. I got the opportunity to sleep in his room on his bed. I wore one of his t-shirts to sleep.

I got to speak as his girlfriend at the memorial service. I had elders paying their respects to me at their house. I was not banished to mourn alone because we were not married. There isn’t anything marriage would have given me that my love didn’t already give me. I was loved till the very end. After everything yesterday his family took me to the farm he’d said would be our wedding venue. His younger brother told me I was his sister for life and made me promise to never, ever leave him. Baby brother, I promise to never, ever leave you.

The family has opened up their home to me and assured me that I am welcome WHENEVER I want to come. I was telling my therapist this morning that I can’t believe my angel man and I really broke all the black families’ rules like we did. We went on SO many holidays together. Something people didn’t understand. How our families would approve of the holidays because we were not married. In fact for our very first getaway a few weeks into our relationship I took him to my mother’s house when she was still in Botswana.

My love and I shared a fairytale love story. Even after his passing this love still surprises me. My therapist and I were laughing at that I’m probably the first girlfriend in the history of black families and loss to be acknowledged like this. Love didn’t die with my angel man. I feel it still even after he’s gone.

I’ve always said I’m the luckiest person you’ll ever meet. I consider myself INCREDIBLY lucky to have experienced that kind of love. My angel gave me forever in our five years together and for that I will ALWAYS be grateful.
