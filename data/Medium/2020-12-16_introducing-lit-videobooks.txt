Introducing LIT Videobooks

Lit Videobooks

Hi, I’m Ronak Sheth, CEO & Founder of LIT. I founded LIT with the mission to make bestselling business books accessible to all with an engaging and fun new format called videobooks.

For years, I’ve spent countless hours reading and listening to hundreds of books, only to forget what I learned weeks later. Contrarily, to this day, I still reference documentaries I’ve seen months, and sometimes, years ago. Visuals stick with me more intensely. In fact, approximately 65% of us are visual learners. I knew there could be a better way to consume and retain the books I love.

I found the answer in working directly with top authors and Emmy award-winning production talent to bring business books to life like never before. Videobooks present the core ideas of each chapter in a 5–10 minute long documentary-like episode. You can now watch a book in under 2 hours, saving you time while helping you to retain more.
