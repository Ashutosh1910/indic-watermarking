Journey’s End — A War movie that inspires you from different perspective

unknown

War Brings tragedy and destruction to people. How the psychological status of soldiers are being ignored?

At the end of 2019, there was a movie about World War I on how British Soldiers defend Britain has sparked the discussion of the public. The movie is called 1917.

After watching 1917, I started feeling interested in history during that period. Therefore, when I found this movie on Netflix, it has aroused my interest.

In 1918, after the war has sparked off for four years. A young Second Lieutenant Raleigh newly arrived at the front and ask to join C company in the trenches. The reason that Raleigh would choose to join C company because the commander Captain Stanhope is a long-time friend from school and to his family.

Instead of having a warm welcome of what Raleigh expected, Captain Stanhope becomes an aggressive and self-alcoholic person. He doesn’t treat Raleigh nicely and even doesn’t want to meet Raleigh. The one that treats Raleigh nicely is Lieutenant Osborne also nickname as “Uncle”. Even when Captain Stanhope is in the bad moon and has a bad temper on him, Lieutenant Osborne will show his understanding of Captain Stanhope. It is because Lieutenant Osborne knows how much stress that Captain Stanhope had throughout these years. He also knows the reason that Captain Stanhope doesn’t want to see Raleigh.

On the night before the day that they finally have to go on the battlefield. Captain Stanhope finally sit down with Raleigh and reveals his inner thought to him. Even though they had prepared four years for this day, hoping this day can come as soon as possible so that they do not have to bear the stress for so long.

When that day comes, though they are still nervous and worried about the day. All of them are still well-prepared to sacrifice for the country including the cook. They know it is a proud responsibility to serve the country and all the things will end soon.

Most of the war films that I have watched are either focused on how the wars bring tragedy to the people or the actions sequences in the movie. In this movie, it does not have a lot of action scene or CGI.

Instead, it focused a lot on what the audience has always ignored, the pressure and the psychological status of soldiers. Even though they are the chosen ones to serve the country or prepared to sacrifice for the country. They are still human, and they would feel scared and worried about the war.

Though I haven’t watched many movies that starred Sam Claflin. In this movie, his performance has changed my impression of him from his previous performances. He plays this aggressive but at the same time have a huge inner thought and pressure buried inside him, I would say this is one of his performance. In addition to that, though Paul Bettany and Toby Jones don’t have many appearances in the movie. Both of them have provided a memorable performance for the audience.
