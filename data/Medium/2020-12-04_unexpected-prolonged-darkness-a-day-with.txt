What Is Sun For Us

The role of Sun in our existential reality is much beyond the light & heat we visibly experience.

It is the largest contributor of energy which all creations use for their survival.

It plays a crucial role in most of the biological and physiological processes around us.

On land & in the ocean, it governs the fueling of plant growth.

It lays the foundation for the food chain.

It adds to the warmth to atmospheric air & controls the weather for living beings & nature.
