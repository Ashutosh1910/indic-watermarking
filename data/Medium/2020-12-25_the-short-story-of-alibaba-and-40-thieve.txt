What is the story of Alibaba and the forty thieves?

Many years ago, a carpet merchant lived in a city in Persia. His 2 sons were Qasim and Alibaba. His business was going well but he died after some time after which the elder son Qasim, who was a greedy man, took over all the business and drove Alibaba home.

After which Alibaba and her wife moved to another place and started living in poverty. Alibaba used to cut wood in the forest and take care of his wife and children.

All this was going on that one day as Alibaba was cutting wood in the forest, he saw 40 horsemen passing through the forest, he was hiding behind a tree and watching it.

Alibaba was very surprised to see all this. He was looking at that mountain for some time like that secret door opened again and forty thieves came out of it and went away, after which the door was closed again.

Alibaba wished to know a lot about what was inside that door. He went to that mountain to learn this and the chieftain of the thieves spoke the words, “Khul Ja Sim Sim”, after that said the door opened again, and went inside the Alibaba cave.

Inside the cave, Alibaba saw that there was a lot of gold, silver, diamonds, gems, and gold coins in the cave. He became very happy to see all this. He understood that all thieves hide their theft and loot in the cave of that hill.

He filled some gold coins in a bundle and came out of that cave. He told all this thing to his wife and showed the coins.

Which made his wife very happy. Alibaba asked his wife to bring the scales to his elder brother Qasim’s house to count the gold coins and said not to tell anyone about it.

Alibaba’s wife then went to Qasim’s house and asked her wife to weigh the scales. Qasim’s wife was a very loving woman.

He suspected that such poor people came to these poor places, then he applied a little glue under the scales and gave it to him. At night, Alibaba and her wife broke the gold coins and in the morning returned the scales to Qasim’s wife.

When Qasim’s wife looked back at the scales, she found a gold coin affixed to it. She told this to her husband. Hearing this, Qasim went to his brother Alibaba and showed sympathy to him by showing sympathy to him. Alibaba told him the whole world with the words “Khul Ja Sim Sim” to open the door.

Qasim, knowing all this, took some mule with him and went to the same hill. He said “Khul Ja Sim Sim” and the door opened, after which he went inside it. He went inside and saw a lot of treasure and filled it up, but as soon as he started speaking that word to get out of that cave, he forgot this word.
