“grandma, I am just playing”- to her angry face.

“no baby, don’t torture the animals or flies, even we should let them live, don’t you want to go to heaven? If you do good deed, then you will go to haven else god will take you to hell” — grandma trying to release the fly from my hand.

“oh, we can plan for it this weekend, we all can go to heaven then”- my 6-year-old brain planning for vacation over the weekend.

“you can go to heaven only when time comes, when you get old and have kids and grandkids”- my grandma kept her point.

“so, you will be going alone?”- cross questioning begins.

“you can go only when you die!”- grandma sighed for my words.

I was curious and want to know more about it.

“grandma! I was about to send that fly to heaven! You stopped me!”- there comes our curious kid.

I know my mom was shocked by my words, thinking what is going on with this kid.

“oh god! Grandma this man will go to hell if he kills him”- my daily commentary changed with context.

“yes, yes he should go to hell he is torturing him from past 1 month”- my grandma was confident.

“what nonsense are you talking?” — go and pray, pointing towards our praying room my grandma concentrated on serial.

I decided will take grandma to hell with us at any cost, because I am Lion 😉
