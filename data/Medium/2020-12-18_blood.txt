Blood

Pusheen Plush

I had awoken to a room full of darkness save for a sliver of light pouring from my window. The window I could have sworn I closed before I slept. After getting up to close it I noticed something on my table. "Huh? A candle?" I muttered under my breathe as I lifted the small crimson candle. Curious, I turned it to read the label. "Ketsueki? What an odd name." I tried to make sense of it. Perhaps it’s in another language. I softly placed it back on my table shrugging it off as a gift from my mother, but as I stepped away I felt a sharp pain on my finger. Apparently the candle was chipped, as my finger had been thinly sliced open. Figuring i should go back to bed I laid back down and closed my eyes only to have the most vivid dream of my entire life. I was completely surrounded by darkness; however, one thing stood out. People... No, things. These things had jet black eyes, and tears of ink. They were staggering over towards me as the ground turned soggy and red. I started to sink burning in the wax like substance. Screaming out in pain, I woke up in a cold sweat. The candle was now on my bed. This time the label was in English. I read it aloud in a shaky tone, "Blood." There was a note next to it this time. It read "A drop to heal any wound." Puzzled, I put the candle in my pocket and continued my day. That’s when it happened. On my walk to my uncles with my best friend Erica a scraggly man stopped us. "We don’t want any trouble, sir." I stuttered trying to hurry around him and dragging Erica with. "Oh, it’s no trouble." He replied in a raspy voice. Silver and red is all I saw next. Suddenly, Erica dropped. Choking on the smell of blood, I looked at the man’s hand. He had a knife, and the blade was about to go inside of me next. Unable to move from fear I was stabbed. He fled the scene and we laid their bleeding. No cars, no houses, and no people for miles. Desperate, I took out the candle and light it with my lighter. I took a breath knowing the sting about to come and poured it on my wound. Success! I felt no more pain. "Erica! We will be fine." I exclaimed joyfully turning to my life long friend. "Erica?.." Dread filled my voice as I realized she was no longer breathing. I quickly poured as much wax as I could on it. Her eyes slowly opened and she gasped for air before coughing blood. Tears filled my eyes as I held her. We kept this a secret, but I noticed Erica seemed more afraid. She wouldn’t stop talking about a demon with melting skin telling her about the Hell she would suffer every night in exchange for her life. I told her not to think about it and went home. Later that night I had another horrible dream. This time... I saw Erica among the shadow people. She was still bleeding from the wound inflicted on her earlier that day. "Does this mean everyone i heal in the future will end up here?" I asked myself. "No... I won’t heal anyone else. Not if it means this." But i was wrong. Sometimes you are so desperate you pay any price. Even if it’s a price someone else has to pay...
