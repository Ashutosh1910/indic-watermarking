I Am the Person That Lives in an Oversized House

Erica N

Hello. My name is Carol and I am a proud owner of a 3600 sqft three-bedroom house. Even though I live alone in this McMansion, it feels great to live in an oversized house.

Some people may ask, why would I live in such a large house? Yes, I live alone but you never know what the future may bring. I may get married and have three beautiful children and poof! everything is taken care of. No need to move to a bigger house because we are already in one. Even though when that day comes, we may end up moving out anyways to get an even bigger house.

That’s the problem with today’s world. Everyone is busy thinking small and thinking of the present. I roll my eyes every time someone tells me how I don’t need that much space.

I bought this giant house just in case I need an 18 room home. You never know when you may actually need all of those rooms.

Why get a one-bedroom home now when your future tells you to get that three-bedroom home for your currently non-existent family?

Why should I buy a small twin size bed when I can get a massive king size bed for me and my currently non-existent husband?

If I don't get what I want, I can always sell my house and get an even bigger house. What if I want four children instead of three? What if I want additional rooms? What about a guest house? What if I want a home with a six-car garage (one car for me, the husband, and for each of the four children for when they grow up)?

You are probably wondering how could I afford all of this? Easy. Ever since I changed jobs and got a pay raise, I bought a bigger house because that’s what a proud American like me is supposed to do right?

I took out a loan to buy a house that I knew I couldn’t afford but I did it anyways. I need to show people proof that I am climbing up the social ladder. As long as I can keep my friends jealous of me I am in a good position.

Sometimes I laugh at myself every time I find myself getting lost in my own house. My house is so large that I sometimes don’t know what to do with it. I have a room for everything.

I have a tea room, a laundry room, a reading room, an office, a room for thinking, a room for standing, a room for sitting, a room for yawning, and a room dedicated to charging my phone (yes, my phone has its own room).

Yes, I have a lot of rooms but those are just spare rooms until my future husband and three kids come along. Once that happens, this house will be thriving in no time.

I don’t think small. I think big and I will continue to buy oversized, overpriced items until I am satisfied (which will be never).

Thank you for understanding.
