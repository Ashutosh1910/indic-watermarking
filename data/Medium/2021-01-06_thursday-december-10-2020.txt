Lukasz Laniecki

Woke up 3:00 am

“A land of promise” or “a promising land” make much more sense than “a promised land”.

Yesterday when I was searching for the new book by Barack Obama I chanced upon the book by Matt Margolis and Mark Noonan entitled The Worst President in History: The Legacy of Barack Obama.

Turns out there are people who believe that Barack Obama was the worst president in the entire history of the USA.

For some it’s Donald, and for others it’s Barack. Who is right and who is wrong?

Most likely nobody.

A week ago or so I chanced upon a Medium post entitled How I’ll make $10,000 With This Single Article On Medium And How You Can Too — Explained by someone called Bola Kwame.

One of the ingredients of the secret sauce according to Bola Kwame (and I think he or she is right) is the title. The story has to get people to click on it. So the title should be a click bait. Whether or not I want to use it as my tactic is a different thing. I agree with Bola that click baits work. The title will stand a better chance of becoming a click bait if you’ll use extremes, says Bola. And again, I agree. He / She explains On the internet, the more extreme content, the big and bold claims, the outliers, that’s what people love to read, that’s the kind of content the internet wants. “How I lost 5 pounds” vs “How I lost 50 pounds”, “How I learned to run 1 mile” vs “How I learned to run a full marathon” Bola says that in each case of the two the latter is more captivating, and would entice you to click.

Now we know why someone would call someone the worst president in history. A title The Worst President in History: The Legacy of Barack Obama is a way better title (from the point of view of the author — how many copies of the book he or she will sell) than for example The Legacy of Barack Obama, or The Mistakes of Barack Obama, or The Unfulfilled Promises of Barack Obama, or even than the following titles Do I Think That Barack Obama Was The Worst President in History? or Why I Believe That Barack Obama Was The Worst President in History? or Could Barack Obama Be The Worst President in History?

Nothing trumps the actual title The Worst President in History: The Legacy of Barack Obama. It doesn’t matter that it’s just someone’s opinion and that it’s not automatically the truth. This person can be the only person in the entire world who has such opinion (hard to believe when there are 1,356 ratings on Amazon, 73% of which are 5 star reviews). But as a title for a book it’s great, I must admit.

That’s how people who care about marketing create the titles. Click baits rule because they work. Unfortunately. The author may actually be a lousy writer or thinker, but if the title’s right there is a greater chance that people will buy his or her shit.
