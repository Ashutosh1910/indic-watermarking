Weird Dream! What if there would be no books in the world??

Neelam Biradar

“A library without books is like a body without a soul.”

It is sometimes is beyond our wonder how our life would be if an essential item be missing out completely. It scared me even though it is just a dream.

Since I was a kid reading was always a part of me and books became an extension of me. As I grew up my stack of books also grew, my spectrum of reading also became wider and brighter. Everyone for once thinks that there is something they want to passover to the upcoming generation. And now looking around me I see kids addicted to their screens I always think books would be a great treasure to pass on the next generation. I hope this thought is echoed and the the number of books and stories that are passed over and that will be passed over grow. Because the stories will always stay with us.

The joy of buying new books, the happiness of finding something worthwhile in an old used books, the stories that get passed on, the stories that stay with us, all this is so much beautiful with us surrounded by books. And its just not the object that brings joy, it gives you knowledge, it helps you travel when you can’t in reality, it becomes a buddy when you are lonely and books just make us better people. I as a reader have grown leaps and bounds as a human. As we grow up our imaginations grow so little but reading just helps you with your imagination- Bonus points.

Stories have always been a part of us whether it was our bedtime stories or the stories we build or the stories we hear.

What a world it would be without books?

This question was always there at the back of my mind but I never bothered to acknowledge it. Now that I think of it, it just sends shivers down my back. What a world we would build ? So much beauty would be lost, so many stories lost, and so many people would be lost. Imagine racks without books, bookshops empty. Our life also would be the same empty. I know its a small habit but sometimes small things bring big changes. Our generations to come would be void of imagination, friendships, stories, love, travel. Their minds would be dry and arid like desert.

Especially this pandemic taught us that in times like these when we become stagnant sometimes our mind and body takes a toll. And by my personal experience it has- movies and entertainment are there but there will always be a limit and in most cases you end up just watching and nothing ends up being a part of you. But with reading it has kept me sane in this insane time. I travelled sitting on my couch, i met new people heard their stories. Just give it a moment’s thought and imagine this all would never be possible if we did not have books.

Time to get up end this bad dream and thank for the existence of books in our lives.
