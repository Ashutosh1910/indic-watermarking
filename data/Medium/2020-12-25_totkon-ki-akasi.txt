Totkon ki Akasi..!

Syeda Ariba

During school days, my class teacher used to keep on repeating this line again and again and this somewhat has stuck with me till now. She used to say and I quote, “ Learning is a never ending process. You students learn from us and we learn from you. There isn’t a single day in my life even at this age that I don’t learn a small chunk everyday.”

At that time I might not have understood the depth of her words but now I surely do! And now while connecting the dots I feel like yes, everything does happen for a reason! On this note, I would like to thank Amal for making me realize it hence giving me a lollipop moment.

Honestly, I have always been a keen learner but I never recognized it. I love trying new things but never could be consistent with it. Amal’s journey up till now and especially these 5 amal totkey have made me realize that how consistency is important and just trying new things is not important but getting out of the comfort zone and doing it is the real deal.

Talking about amal totkas, I loved each and every totka! Starting with the very first tip ‘Self Talk’, there are times when I just get so unmotivated whenever I can’t make something happen and negativity just takes over me. At that time this tip helped me to get myself hyped and focus on the positives. Self reflection and then keeping a positive mindset really helped me get through the day.

Secondly, getting out of my comfort zone has always been challenging for me even when I try so there are things that just hold me back. But through this course I really have learnt the importance of not getting used to the same seat and I am a strong believer that all the opportunities lie outside my bubble hence I have tried to overcome it for this purpose I have implemented the third totka ‘Create new habits’. I try to create new habits that will actually drive me towards my goals. For starters, I have put alarms throughout the day to remind of the activities and I try to complete them on time.

The fourth tip has helped me in understanding my strengths and weaknesses, collecting feedback from my teachers, parents and colleagues has led to some major improvements in my personality. On collecting feedback, I got to know that I am too impatient duh! Now literally I am practicing to be more patient and my friends are even helping me out to do so *blessed*.

Lastly comes the pro tip ‘Fake it till you make it’, I haven’t implemented it but I would love to! Even Aristotle said: “Men acquire a particular quality by constantly acting a certain way.” So yes, I will try to adopt this habit and hope it will contribute to my personality in a positive way.

This was all for my reflection, I loved the amal totkas and I’m really looking forward to implementing these in my personal and professional life.
