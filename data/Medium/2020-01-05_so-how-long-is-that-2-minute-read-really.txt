So How Long Is That 2 Minute Read Really Going to Take?

Robert Turner

How do you actually work out how long it takes me to read for two minutes.

Enough already. I have a collection of foods in my refrigerator telling me how long I need to cook them for, by when I should cook them and then there’s the health warnings. Literally telling me how long I will have after consumption of said fatty, cholesterol-laden delights before death sets in.

I have numbered steps telling me that I am only four away from happiness, or possibly five. I don’t quite understand step three.

Once I have achieved happiness I can then go back to the book on Success. Happiness was a requirement for success, I think Step 5 if memory serves.

As if that’s not enough, now I am told how long it will take to read? I intensely dislike being told what to do or how long I have to do it in. I am the ultimate intolerant 52-year-old kid when it comes to authority. Or perhaps I just recognize a rubbish idea when I see one.

Shouldn’t we be driven by the subject matter and to hell with the length? I thought that’s what the reading list was for.

Anyway, I now feel obliged to sit with a stopwatch and time my reading. What happens if I go over? Am I a poor reader? What if I finish in under the allotted time. Do I win something? Where is the pop-up with the free iPhone? Wait, maybe I missed a part. Damn, I’ll have to go through it again.

I now studiously avoid any read over five minutes, the pressure is just too much. Oh, and that’s the other thing. Who, pray tell, benchmarks the reading speed. Is there even such a thing. Do they use the Flash or that sloth from Zootopia?

Do they have a panel of readers all reading simultaneously and an average time is used, or is it just one person? If it’s just one, how is he selected?

Is his eyesight above average, does he wear glasses and did he pass elementary reading at grade school? How tall is he? Wait. What’s tall got to do with reading? Well for a start the person is further away from the text than a shorter person. The permutations are dizzying. These blog sites must employ a gaggle of statisticians.

I wonder how they are selected?

Actually, I’ve just had another thought whilst contemplating all of this. Does the two minutes include the time required to put on your glasses and/or zoom in on pictures? Maybe they could add a viewing time for images, say ten seconds per and I’d need at least an extra five to find my glasses. Bloody things are always missing.

I read today that Medium employs an algorithm to check the speed an article is browsed. Too fast and it’s not considered reading. More pressure. It’s all too much.

I feel a headache coming on. I think I will go and find my pain relief tablets.The sixty second ones.
