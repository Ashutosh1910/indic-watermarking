“Dolly Parton’s Christmas on the Square” Movie: An In-Depth Love Analysis

Sara Knick

I visited my dad’s side of the family yesterday and my cousin put on a movie that had just come out this year. It was called, “Dolly Parton’s Christmas on the Square.” It was filled with a lot of twists and turns. The main character in the movie, Regina, got pregnant when she was younger and the baby was given up for adoption. She was not accepted in this town. She was originally going to sell the town to profit off of a business opportunity. She found out who the baby grew up to be and there was a happy ending.

She desired career success, money and wealth. There was a lot of unresolved conflict from her past that disconnected her from feeling a connection with people in the town. She didn’t get to be with the man that she loved and this caused her sadness that she buried inside of her.

She couldn’t feel empathy for any of the town members because she was already feeling that she was not accepted in the town to a large degree. She was going to sell the town because it did not mean anything to her. There was no connection or meaning that she valued from it.

When we feel hurt, we can either choose to heal ourselves or choose to hurt others. This main character, Regina, was not fully healed from the past. There was no resolve about what had happened in her younger years. She continued to hurt others emotionally by being disconnected from them, showing a lack of empathy and acting for her own selfish gain.

Regina wanted to sell the town. This was what she came to the town to do originally. The angel and the angel-in-training in the movie helped her to change her heart and her mind. She connected with her son, who was the pastor of the town, and decided not to sell the town after all. The town had meant something to her by the end of the movie because she reassigned new meaning to it.

Regina kept having flashbacks to these feelings of being alone and unaccepted in love during her high school years.

The movie had shown that when we get hurt, we can continue hurting ourselves and others if we don’t get resolve. In love, she was hurt and disappointed. She knew that she was not accepted and acted in ways that were not heart-centered. She used control so that she could obtain power, money and wealth. She did not consider her own happiness until the angel in the movie brought it up.

At the beginning of the movie, she was determined to sell off the town. She associated this town with negativity and hurt because of her past. Throughout the movie, she reminisced over memories of the town and what the town meant to her so that by the end of the movie, she decided to not sell off the town after all. In love, we should strive to connect to our true selves and our authenticity as well as act in ways that are heart-centered. This is where we will find happiness.
