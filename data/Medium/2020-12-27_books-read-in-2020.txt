Books Read in 2020:

Tiffanie Snyder

Little Fires Everywhere by Celeste Ng (If you have not watched the TV series, I recommend reading the book first).

Conviction by Denise Mina. A fast-paced and fun murder mystery.

Postscript by Cecilia Ahern. It made me cry. A lot.

I’ve Been Thinking by Maria Shriver. This book kind of freaked me out because she wrote it as if she kind of knew Covid was going on…even though she wrote it in 2018. It’s inspiring and filled with wisdom, so it was perfect timing that I read it in March.

Juliet, Naked by Nick Hornby. I read it, and then I watched the movie. And of course, the book was better.

Lessons by Gisele Bundchen. I liked this book a lot more than I thought I would. It really made me appreciate Mother Nature more.

City of Girls by Elizabeth Gilbert. This was really cute and I loved it a lot.

Touched by the Sun by Carly Simon. She really didn’t talk about Jackie O as much as I thought she would, so this one was kind of a let down.

Woman in the Window by A.J. Finn. This one was so good. I want the movie to come out already please.

The Defining Decade by Meg Jay. This one makes you think. A lot.

Feel Free by Zadie Smith. This one was good.

Three Wishes by Liane Moriarty. This one was really cute and I loved it and I want someone to make a movie from it with the Olsen sisters.

The Jetsetters by Amanda Eyre Ward. It wasn’t as good as I thought it would be.

Queens of Animation by Nathalia Holt. If you want to learn a lot and love Disney, I recommend this one.

Sex and Vanity by Kevin Kwan. This is the perfect beach read. If you liked Crazy Rich Asians, you’ll like this one too.

Sisters First by Jenna and Barbara Bush. This book was adorable and I loved that they didn’t really go in chronological order.

How Could She by Lauren Mechling. This one is supposed to be what The Undoing is based off of, which is why I read it. But I forgot basically the entire book by the end of the year. Yikes.

Wordslut by Amanda Montell. This one is quite awesome and it really makes you think about the power of language.

Midnight Sun by Stephanie Meyers. This made me want to rewatch Twilight the whole time. It’s a really long book.

The Proposal by Jasmine Guillory. It was cute.

Beach Read by Emily Henry. I liked this one because it was about writers.

Little Black Book by Otegha Uwagba. Very inspiring and great for anyone who is creative or in the work force!

Everyone Worth Knowing by Lauren Weisberger. It was okay. The ending was cute.

I Remember Nothing and other Reflections by Nora Ephron. I laughed out loud. A lot.

The Home Edit Life by Clea Shearer and Joanna Teplin. This one is sooo aesthetically pleasing if you are looking to re-organize any space. Also if you love a rainbow color scheme, you cannot go wrong with this book.

Normal People by Sally Rooney. It’s good, but there’s no punctuation. Grammar lovers beware.

Greenlights by Matthew McConaughey. I really love this man and I really loved his book.

What I Like About You by Marisa Kanter. It’s cute, but insults all adults who read YA throughout the entire book.

Useless Magic by Florence Welch. I like her poetry. If you like her music or poetry, read it.

Breakfast at Tiffany’s by Truman Capote. I didn’t realize it’s only 87 pages! But what a magnificent 87 pages it is. TOP BANANA as Capote says.

2021 Reading Goals: I want to reflect after each book. Write down my favorite quotes. I want to learn a lot. I want to read books that impact my life a lot.
