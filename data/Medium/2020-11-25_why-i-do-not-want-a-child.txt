Why I do not want a child

Seifert Han 韓博鈞

My junior sister gave birth to her daughter last month. Baby always seems the cutest and most beautiful one in the world. Every animal’s childhood is very adorable also. Many of my friends have been married and have babies, except a few of them working abroad perennially. Every once in a while, someone shows the babies’ photos in the IM group. People my age may accustom to this kind of “ceremony” in life. However, the so-called “people” never include my wife and me.

Before marriage, we had reached an agreement not to have children. The reason is about our attitude toward lifestyle but without economic affordability. Both of us are the persons who love to be footless and fancy-free. I am impatient and always thinking about many more ideas about innovations or start-ups. Amy, as my wife and soulmate, very concerned about peace in daily living. Our characters have spontaneous mindsets and are also unwilling to be restrained by anything as much as possible.

Even since the first day of pregnancy, having a baby is backbreaking work for parents. Besides my sister, countless cases have proved the point of view. We have to note that attentively: an infant is incredibly easily injured. Father and Mother, even the whole family, have to take care of the newborn 24/7. Most of the little bairns need to feed many times day and night, and no matter how parents are tired, they have to focus on the moppet at any time. Crying and all kinds of noises by the cherub become background sounds of the everyday. Honestly, we are both afraid of it.

A child will grow up gradually according to your words, deeds, and teaching because they are a living human even though the child will form his or her thoughts or wills. As their learning ability increases, not only do they understand more but also ask more. From childhood to adolescence, kids will be affected by their living environments and information in daily life. If we expressed the wrong or dark side of ourselves, they would learn and internalize it as a part of their personality. Although parents unintentionally show lousy behaviors or let them know improper information accidentally, tots always assimilate what they acknowledge anytime and anywhere. Once people ignore any mind developing period, children will go astray on the road of life. Compare with mediocrity, becoming destructive is more worried.

Friends or relatives know our thinking ask us what should we do if no one takes care of us when we are old. We believe that raise a child not for preventing oneself from aged helpless. Selfless and sincere love is the main reason for raising children. Maybe Amy and I would have a kid someday; it must be the cause of eagerness to contribute our passion or something positive to the innocent and the world. Before fate coming, we still enjoy the life of two. Sharing readings, talking to each other, and starting an indiscriminate trip seems much more attractive yet.
