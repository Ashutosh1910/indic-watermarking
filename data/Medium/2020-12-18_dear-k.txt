Dear K.,

Waiting For You

It’s the second letter in the series. Although its begening of the end but I can help myself to tell you the things that I find worth telling.

Two important things happened this week. One was well published, the other did die dow un the alleys of data points.

The news of rollout of vaccine received widespread coverage, but the release of first phase of NFHS-5 data did no attract much columns. Both had significant insights on the nature of future we might have in the coming days.

Vaccine will make us free but the data of NFHS 5 makes me worried about the future we are going to have. The mot important takeaway from the NFHS 5 data is the declining fertility rate with only three states above the replacement level fertility rates i.e. Bihar, Meghalaya and Manipur. This mean the population of India will stabilise by at max 2045 with upper limit of 1600 million. This is going to have profound effects on the economy and lifestyle. Demand will slowdown in most sectors except healthcare and entertainment. India will face the middle income trap with growth rarely crossing 5% level(caveat being the next year due to low base effect).

What this means to people like me if I would have to continue in the same service… Declining revenues mean that my department will be forced to work over time. More dealers will be brought under scrutiny and assessment. Declining tax revenues will force govt to cut more spending from health and education. Hence low level of human development will lead to low quality of human capital formation, which is already affected by the pandemic(India has slipped in HDI rankings). It will widen the gap between the rich and the poor, leading to law and order problems. We are already witnessing this in Bihar.

How come it may relate us?? We will be at the extremes of the the social sphere of revenue generation. Your profession will accumulate more wealth due to more geriatric care, and lifestyle diseases. My profession will be to extract more revenue fro the wealth. Both our professions will have insatiable demands. It is estimated that almost 20% of the population in 2045 will be above 60 years. The revenue demand of govt will cross 100 lakh crore. I don’t know what will be system of tax collection in 2045 but it will be surely more efficient than the techniques used now.

All this will imply that my breed of people will be detested everywhere especially in the rich circles. But there is a contrasting scenario too. What if I leave this job and join any service which is related to law and order. We may complement each other. Unfortunately it is jus wishful thinking. Rest assured I will get more distant in future.

I have no demands from you that you ever read this letter. I have learnt to live with your neglect of my feelings. I only hop that I remain alive long enough to write all the things that I wish you knew.
