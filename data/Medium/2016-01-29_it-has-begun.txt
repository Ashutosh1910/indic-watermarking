It Has Begun

Shalom Gauri

The clouds rode the wind and raced across the fading strip of sky, squeezing between the dark building that leaned in on me. The sun looked uncomfortable in his seat, shifting constantly, causing shadows to leap from wall to wall, pouncing on innocent windows. Maybe he was simply restless…like the rest of us. Everything around me hurried past, failing terribly in the act of business.

I turn the corner and enter a bleak street. It is devoid of color except for the flowers on window sills, that wished not to droop, but couldn’t care to stand straight and tall. Among these crouching flowers, there appears to be a face peering straight out at me. Her eyes follow me drearily, allowing not a single expression to flicker across her bored face. I quicken my pace and leave her behind. The trees looked ruffled and baffled, swaying awkwardly and the leaves underfoot crunch complainingly, grumbling like cranky children.

I sigh. Not because I’m sad, not because I’m depressed, but because of having nothing better to do. *Sigh*. The city sighs with me and for a moment everything stops. Silence, stillness, nothing more. Everything, everything in the entire universe pauses. Except for Time. Time never stops.

Tick-tock. Tick-tock. Tiicck — toock…and it began. Finally began. The rain.

Deep down inside, we were all waiting for it. Foot-hopping-ly, nail-bitingly, hair-tearing-ly waiting for it. The skies break into a smile, restlessness and frustration is sucked up into the clouds.

To some the sky was crying. Time enough to sit sorrowfully by the window or to trudge broken-hearted away from your partner… never turning to look back. To others it was something to be forgotten. Open an umbrella, and get on with life! To many others it was a time for joy. Not the kind of joy that bursts out, erupts in the air and ends in song. No… this joy was different. This was a suppressed joy. A “smile-to-myself” kind of joy.

The mud and leaves squelched noisily and happily, no longer flattened out, but simply pressed into the ground. The stones glistened a soft grey-blue, standing out among the fallen leaves of burnt colours. The music of the trees fills the air, as they pass raindrops from leaf to leaf, swaying in the goose-bumping wind. Flowers of all colours now unite in a un-nameable shade. They stand straight and light on their lacy stems, and a thin sheet of mist gathers itself around them. The city is now spotted with innumerable mirrors of puddles, aflame with the merging red and orange headlights. Up above, the clouds too are one, hiding the sun but letting through selective rays of shimmering, golden dust. It is like a transparent curtain, creating a light show for the gods.

The world lies wet and quiet. The smell of awakened earth weaves itself into the mist, which rests warm and thick like a blanket, subduing the constant beeping of traffic, calming and relaxing a once restless city… the rains have arrived.
