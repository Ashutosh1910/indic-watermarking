Sakshi’s performance at her institute Aangikam, which was aired at DD National.

Sakshi Nautiyal, a 12th grade student of ITL Public School, who has been a Kathak dancer for 13 years now, narrates her experience with dancing and the impact of dance on various aspects of her life. She articulates how dance has always been something she can fall back on, if she stumbles.

Sakshi Writes.

“If you ever stumble , just make it a part of your dance.

So I’m not going to give some clichéd reason that I started Kathak because of it’s elegance or the way the dancer’s body moved that attracted me. Rather, I was just a 4 year old whose mother did not know what to do with her, when her elder sister had to be taken to music lessons. The more I started learning and understanding the nuances of this form, the more I fell in love with it. Recently I joined an institute to gain better professional training in Kathak from Pandit Ram Mohan Maharaj and my dance journey changed for the good.

Sakshi performing at her school’s cultural function

A lot of people my age face issues like bullying and body image issues. A very prominent one for me, was the fear of losing people, as I did lose someone too close to my heart, however then dance taught me that I am a whole.

Kathak for me has been a constant, something I know I can always fall back on in times of stress and anxiety. I’ve always faced terrible anxiety before performing but almost 13 years of performing on stage now, dancing has really helped me calm myself. It’s like everytime I’m on stage, its just me and my dance and no worries.

Being a dancer I’ve had a disciplined life. From travelling an hour to Mandi House just for practices to little things like walking straight. My parents have been always supportive of me and believed in my dance, they always took me to practices, even if it meant skipping their own work.

Her dance follows her everywhere she goes

A very low phase in my life was a period of two years where I was terribly ill. Amidst the doctor visits it felt like my life was not going return to normalcy but being a dancer has given me both mental and physical strength. Soon I started going back to my classes and little by little getting back to my old self. Kathak has given me good immunity which I really lacked years ago. Even when I got my ankle fractured, which is a nightmare for every dancer, I did not lose hope. Kathak taught me that even in such times, when you do stumble, you are strong enough to get up.

For me, when everyone at my school knows me by the name of “the kid who dances at every event”, somewhere in my heart I believe that dance has made me the person I am , that it helped me make a place for myself.

Sakshi gracefully taking the stage with her Kathak performance

Helping others the way dance helped me is a dream come true. A dream in which this organisation, Avaaz, plays a huge role.
