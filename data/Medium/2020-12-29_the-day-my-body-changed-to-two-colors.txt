The day my Body Changed to two Colors

Juliano Righetto

I had my first girlfriend at seventeen. We were together for a year and a half, and at that time, all we wanted to do was sex. Of course, we did other things, too… But these things were always an excuse to go to bed sooner or later.

I don’t remember using my brain at this time. Hormones took over me.

On one occasion, my best friend invited us to go to a party on one beach on São Paulo’s North Coast, the state where I live. My friend was a musician and was starting his DJ career, much less common in the eighties. It would be the first party he would work on. I talked to my girlfriend:

ME — We can go to the Mello party, spend the night there, and the next day we go to the beach in Boiçucanga!

SHE — But what time are we going to have sex?

ME — We can sleep in the car… Just find a place isolated enough that we can do whatever we want!

These were our plans.

But it was my brain that came up with the plans… And soon, the hormones came into action.

Instead of going to my friend’s party, we went straight to Boiçucanga, the beach where we would sleep. And we look for somewhere to park the car. I had a Parati at that time; to be very specific, it was the car below:

Photo provided by the Author

As you can see, it was a station wagon, a type of car with a huge trunk. When lowering the rear seats, we had a double bed at our disposal!

So we found an excellent place to park, lowered the seat, and let the hormones take over. We spent the night having fun until it was very late — about four in the morning, I imagine. We were tired; we needed to sleep…

We did it.

Or, at least, we’ve tried to do it.

In a short time, the heat we had generated with our teenage activities, together with breathing in an enclosed space, made the air start to suffocate us.

I opened the glass a crack…

And a swarm of mosquitoes invaded the car!

We had no exciting options. Either we left the glass a little open so that we could breathe, and we died without blood, or we closed the glass and died of suffocation.

This dilemma lasted for several minutes… Until we noticed that the day started to dawn. It was then that I had a brilliant idea:

ME — Let’s sleep on the beach! So the wind won’t let the mosquitoes bite us, and we can breathe!

When the brain took over, it had no good ideas, as you can see…

We did what I thought. We went to the beach, spread out two towels, lay down… And we passed out from sleep.

I was the first to wake up at four in the afternoon.

The beach was full. I imagine that, if this had happened today, we would have been photographed by several people. I got up, dizzy… And I noticed that my girlfriend, who had white skin, was red. I woke her up:

ME — Look at you!!! You’re red!!!

SHE —Look at you!!! You won't believe in your condition!
