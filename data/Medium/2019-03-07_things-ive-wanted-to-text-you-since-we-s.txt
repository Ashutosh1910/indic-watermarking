Things I’ve Wanted to Text You Since We Stopped: A Week in My Thoughts

J Kuiper

i took 14 pictures of art at SFMOMA that are works i think you would like, mostly because i like them

living out of a suitcase must be getting to me… i just spent more money on clothes than i have in years. now we will see if i have an anxiety attack about it and return them all tomorrow

i have a date monday night with a guy i met in december. i guess it counts as a date two?

all this week i will walk past the place where i finally kissed the man i have often felt is the closest thing to “the one,” but that kiss was over a year ago, and we don’t speak, so he’s not — and i don’t notice it in the course of the day really, only when i stop to pay attention to where i am

i wonder if i will actually start to try to have emotions and feelings like i said i would this year or if it’s too late to come back from shutting them down

i am having the best vegetable tagine for lunch

i’m got some good dog time today — golden retriever therapy

i wear my scarf and hat indoors all the time now, breaking my own rules of warmth and clothing. i think of you when i notice it, but not with any real feeling

the “deafening teal” card is, in fact, now a bookmark as you predicted

i’ve spent the morning ear-searching for accents. i don’t have any idea why, it’s not like i go talk to people when i hear them, but i do start eavesdropping some

weather report: very drizzly but neither cold nor warm.

it’s a lovely lazy day of wandering and reading and drinking coffee — my favorite urban type of sunday

i bought three tops at goodwill. i always feel bad when shopping there since i can afford retail. makes me feel like i’m stealing clothes from people who actually need the bargain

and now i’ve bought a pair of shoes. with heels. because fuck it…

i told my sisters about the thought that being in a suitcase for so long has made me feel a sense of urgency for new stuff — when i did, it also occurred to me that this sense of urgency might be a combination of the suitcase limitation and of being rejected?

i wonder if you only knowing me as a person living out of one suitcase made any difference in who you think i am, reduced my dimensionality or complexity somehow?

i’m liking the book i’m reading more than others i’ve read by this author, though i still find her characters to lack something that draws me to caring about them

weather report: still grey, still drizzling, still just barely chilly

it has officially been a week. i’ve officially heard nothing from you, so this note will become journal, and i’m deleting your number, because, well, who are you to me anyway at this point?

it’s funny how i know i can turn my back on someone i spent a decade with, never to speak to them again. how i can understand my own ability to detach and go — and yet i still wonder how it’s so easy for you to say you are my friend and yet ghost completely (as you promised wouldn’t happen)… human promises… i guess they’re really as thin as the air they’re spoken with
