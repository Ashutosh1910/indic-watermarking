Ann had composed herself. She was going to be alright. Despite some previous complications concerning boys she had assured herself that nothing of that sort mattered. She felt like she owed it to Matt to help him. But what had he ever done for her? It was just the whole idea that she liked. Having someone to hang out with, to talk to, someone to love.

Waking up in the night. Wanting to talk. It just makes you feel asdfghjkl.

Ann gathered her things and headed towards the big apple tree. It sat in her front yard. But the branches shaded everything enough that no one could tell you were in there. It was a great place just to sit and watch. You could really see the world go by. People watch- if you will. Listening to the noises of the world around makes you feel a certain type of way. Ann slung her nag over her shoulder and grabbed onto a branch. she pulled herself up until she could get a foot on a branch.

She rocked back and forth on the branch, swinging her feet in the air. Trying not to think too much. She really liked him. But he wasn’t worth it. Too much drama, it was dragging her down. Like he was the weight in her stomach that made her feel an unnecessary need to vomit. But she loved every moment she spent with him.

Ann later had written a letter. It was addressed to you know who. Gross.

It was just a little crush. Feelings don’t really matter, do they? Just repress! Nothing lost then! You’ll just never know what could of been.

Really, this was a mistake. These things always end, there’s no point. But she loved him.

He was supposed to meet her here. But she wasn’t doing very well with this overwhelming stress, should she tell or not? Ann whipped her brow. Her forehead was getting sweaty, and her hand clammy.

Matt was on her mind. Ann’s phone started ringing. The vibrations made the branch her bag was on wiggle a bit. Ann tried to reach over and grab it, but she was unsuccessful, pushing her bag- and all of her belongings off and out of the tree. Matt was under. Looking up he saw her items falling. She saw him and then he was hit in the head with her freshly bitten apple core.

“Hey”

“Fancy seeing you here”

“Ann didn’t you wanna talk? Come on let me join you in the tree!”

He tried to reach towards a branch, but as soon as his feet left the ground he took a tumble.

“Hey!!! Don’t come up here!”

Ann was worried. The letter was in her bag. Her bag that was about fifteen feet below her sitting at the base of the tree right below his feet.

“You, you should leave!”

“But? I just got here? You wanted to talk to me right?”

Ann didn’t want to talk to him right now. The moment was ruined. Why should she even be the one to tell him how she feels. Depending how you look at it, it could be like she was losing. He should be the one to confess to her. She didn’t want to risk anything. It wasn’t worth it.
