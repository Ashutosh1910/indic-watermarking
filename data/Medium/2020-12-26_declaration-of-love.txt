Declaration Of Love

Alejandro Velazquez

To: My Light

A love letter may well be the most unique and influential piece of writing one could create and give to another in these modern days, for a letter requires that the writer be genuine and thoughtful with no words unwritten. I know that my side of our relationship has not always been the most reliable, and in fact, quite confusing, the truth is that I tend to manifest situations from my own doing just so I may have an excuse to hurt myself mentally and then fix it. I don’t ever want to do that to you or myself ever again my love, and yes i mean you too, because when I suffer, you suffer. Nowadays I truly prefer to push my selfishness and emotions aside, so I can accept the fact that you may well be the greatest person to have ever entered my life, and I believe the only way to express how much I appreciate you is by dedicating my time and goals to us, meaning whatever we do, we do together.

Now that I have made that clear; let me explain why I love you, your heart, Such a heart like yours I was of not knowing could exist, a soul that hugs so warmly and lovingly, like a cuddling puppy that came into my life, guiding me to peace. I have much to achieve in life and I am grateful to have at least come across your beautiful life-force. Alongside us, I believe we carry a uniquely strong bond that no matter how much we stretch and tear, it stays intact, considering the shocking fact that we do not argue or find red flags in our relationship horrible enough to tear us apart. Although we have had red flags, when I consider how much time has passed with everything we have dealt with, I am reminded on how stupid the little problems can be and I believe that I have learned to not overthink it. I learned to stop worrying about the bad little things and instead embrace the good little things.

Lastly I appreciate you and your understanding nature. Although it can be a little naive, I love how you have a strong sense to hold on to us in the toughest times, for many relationships do not hold on very tightly, with Laura I held on tightly and it hurt me, hence explaining how easily I would let go of us, even though I knew I did not want to, but because of your strong hold on us, I was able to make mistakes and learn from it, and never repeat it again once I have learned it.

I wrote this letter, not so you can cry, or just like it, I truly wrote this letter so that you may understand that I do love you and why. Hopefully my message was delivered.

P.S YOU TRULY ARE THE BLESSING IN MY LIFE AND I WOULD LIKE TO KEEP IT THAT WAY.
