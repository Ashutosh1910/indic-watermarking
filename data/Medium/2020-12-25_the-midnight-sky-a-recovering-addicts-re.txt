The Midnight Sky — A (Recovering)Addict’s review

The Recovering Reviewer

Before we go into the movie review — let’s be honest with ourselves about two things, the only reasons we even considered watching this movie are

George Clooney — It does not matter which gender you conform to, George has occupied a special place in our collective hearts for a while now — I mean who can forget Frank Ocean or the movie where he flew around a lot and educated us about frequent flier miles

Its 2020 and we (literally) do not have anything else to watch on Netflix — so we put a silent reminder on the app a couple weeks back when the recommendation algorithm at Netflix ran out of options

Now that we have confessed our true nature — we can begin with the review.

The plot — Well its there, I am certain of it — George promised me that it was in the trailer. Now if you are like me and like watching cinema / movies that leaves you with some dirt in your eyes (Sniff!) — I’ll be honest — this is not that movie. Not is it a movie that rocks your brain and makes you want to start reading high school physics or math. The question then — what is it? The answer — Well it is something, the plot moves in two spaces — Earth which is suffering from some form of human stupidity (I assume — the writers never bothered to explain) and space where a crew of some barely character developed people decide on baby names. On earth George gives a powerful performance that reminds you of good old Leo in “The Revenant” minus the bears and forest, plus some cold, a kid and a snow mobile. The people on earth can’t talk to the guys in space — because well — all of them are dead. Except for George who probably is the only smart guy in the entire planet (Again an assumption) and the genius that he is — George (yeah I don’t remember the character’s name) will find an antenna — Yes its 2049 and that’s the only way to talk to the crew. The ending is the only point in the movie where you see any form of genuine emotion and make this movie just barely watchable

The acting — Well there was some, George did a bit and the people in space that played rummy did some too — I guess. Anybody else that tried barely seemed to have a script that would let them. There a few months strewn about to introduce some drama — but add very little to the movie

Summary — In case you are watching this for the same reasons I did then congratulations — you had something over the holiday period stuck in this pandemic to take a few hours from your life. If you haven’t already and really want to watch movie that moves your brain from that sleep — I recommend a rerun of Interstellar because this is not it.

While I say this this is the first movie I have ever written and for a guy that is only writing this as way to reduce the amount of time he spends watching Tv my opinions may seem strong but are still mine. Also I hope our dear George never does read this — because as I said — He does occupy a special place in my heart
