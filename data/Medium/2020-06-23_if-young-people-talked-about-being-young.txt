If Young People Talked About Being Young The Way Old People Talk About Being Old

Alex Baia

When you’re young like me, you appreciate the big things in life. Lit parties. Your fam. Bangin’ a hottie. It’s hard to explain. It’s just different when you’re young.

Nothing hurts. My back feels great. My knees work perfectly. I can run up and down stairs for hours. I spring out of bed each day feeling awesome. Being young slays.

Life, to me, is like a fine White Claw: tasty for a sec, then you grab another. Keep going till you’re all fucked up.

I’m so young, I remember Minecraft. I remember Gangnam Style. I even remember Harambe. But not Nirvana, Seinfeld, or 9/11. That’s ancient history, gramps.

When you get young, things change. You’re bad with money and you want to have wild sex while screaming “LOLZ! Yeet!!” You’ll understand it when you’re young.

Back when I was growing up, in Obama’s second term, we didn’t write some boring letter to our crush. We texted a dank meme of a ferret twerking while the word AWESOME flashed across its ass. That’s how you show your babe you care.

Jesus, look at my hair! So shiny. So fleek. I’m so damn young.

They don’t make ’em like they used to. My new iPhone 11 can do 4K slow-mo selfies, but my old Samsung Galaxy is a real gross piece of shit.

I miss my love, Susie. I miss her every day. But she’s in a better place right now… NYU.

Let me explain something to you about how young I am: I can do four shots of Tito’s, smoke weed, and eat pizza at 2 am, then wake up the next day and go to class, no cap.

The 80s, man! The 80s were incredible. I saw Stranger Things, then read about it on Vice.

When you’ve lived as few years as I have, you really learn to take your parents for granted and trash your body. Deal with the fallout later. Fuck shit up. No limits, biatch.

Gas used to cost $4 a gallon! Can you believe it? $4! That’s enough to pay off 0.003% of the student loans I took out Freshman year!

You oldsters might have more years under your belts, but we youngsters know a thing or two about the shit that goes down when you slide into those DMs.

You want to talk to me about doctors? Yikes. I don’t go to doctors. I guess I’m just young.

What’s the world coming to? Gay people can marry in Alabama! We can 3D print prosthetic hands! Some things just keep getting better!

When you’re young like me, you start to see that Spring Break isn’t just a seven-day vacation in late March… it’s a state of mind.

The 70s were unreal. Literally nothing compares to hearing David Bowie for the first time, live in the theater, during Guardians of The Galaxy.

Can you believe old people these days? They have all the cash, but they spend it on cringe couches and dumb ashtrays their cats piss in. Old money is wasted on the old.

You want to know what I regret the most? Oof. Weird question, bruh. I don’t think about my regrets. I’m young.
