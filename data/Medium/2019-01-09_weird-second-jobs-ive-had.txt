Weird Second Jobs I’ve Had

Lisa Martens

Because I was smart enough to get into NYU but not smart enough for a full ride.

I just wanted to go to school without dying. Photo credit .https://stocksnap.io/photo/EQ3Q18SVL9

Watching cartoons while getting an MRI as part of a medical experiment.

Another medical experiment on memory that I…don’t quite remember.

Handing out fliers for knockoff perfumes near Grand Central Station.

Being a stagehand for a play about the founding fathers and porn. I can’t sew so I stapled the costumes together. The show must go on.

Selling sunglasses for a guy who was likely using the business as a front for illegal activity. He was always driving to Newark. He hid bags of money around the store. I quit when he asked for my social security number.

Making sure a boom box worked for belly dancing classes.

Line editing manuscripts for a publishing house, and going through the slush pile for gold. Boomers, it’s broke millennials referring your manuscripts to the real editors, so be nice.

Almost a job: while asking bars if they needed extra help, a gentleman told me if I needed cash we could go back to his house and “see what happens.”

I ran away.

Standing outside in December for the NYC holiday market. There’s no bathroom for staff. You just have to run to Whole Foods and hope no one steals from your shop while you panic crap.

Bike tour guide. At the time, I did not know how to ride a bike. A friend of mine hired me as an “independent contractor” to circumvent normal hiring practices.

Weekend tour guide of Governors Island.

Bike tour guide again, this time in Harlem. Quit after it became apparent tourists would die trying to cross 125th on a bike.

Repairing and cleaning Civil War-era weaponry.

Writing press releases for a chiropractic college (that were never published).

Tutoring college students with learning disabilities without being allowed to know what those disabilities were. One frustrated student picked me up.

Selling silver coins to men I met on the Internet. (That was my emergency plan.)

Proofreading stories for people off Upwork. One person (white) wrote a holiday anthology and made a race joke about Black Friday. They got defensive when I suggested removing the race joke. I never heard from them again.

Reselling my clothes and clothes other people threw away.

Same, but with furniture.

Trying to write a Wikipedia article about someone who didn’t really do anything, and getting it rejected because the only source was a book the guy wrote himself.

Taking photos of political bobble heads that were kept in a storage unit. Hundreds of them. Do you know what it is like to have hundreds of smiling bobble head dolls (Of Obama, the Clintons, the Bushes, Saddam, Osama) staring at you in a dark room?

Almost a job: On my way back from my tour guide job, a dude told me he could pay me to be a dominatrix, and that I’d be good because I looked sweet and innocent.
