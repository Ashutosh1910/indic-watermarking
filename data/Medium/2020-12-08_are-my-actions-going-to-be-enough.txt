Are My Actions going to be Enough?

The overall theme for the next few days will become familiar. I am hoping the topics will allow me to delve deeply into different issues of the topic. Today’s prompt is “What makes life feel so complicated right now?” One of the lessons that is learned in life is, I only have control over me. I can control my actions. I can control my reactions. I can control my responses. In my growth this year that is something that I have focused on.

I have used that to deal with and heal past traumas. I have used that to present a better, more grounded, more positive, and more polite person. I have lots more work to do on it but I am happy with my progress so far. This one skill has improved my mindset. This one skill has improved my life. This one thing has improved my world. People that I interact with are more positive. They know they will see a smile. They know there will be a positive comment. The world, rather my world is a better place.

There is one thing about life right now that is the issue. Up until now this has been enough. This past year, these past couple months, that is not enough. Because of the pandemic, my life is also dependent on the actions of others. Right now in the United States there are more new cases reported daily than ever before. Eleven months into a pandemic that we know how it spreads. Eleven months into a pandemic that we know how to control the spread of. I should say, the way I wish to live my life is dependent on the actions of others. I can control my actions. I could decide to not leave my house until I go get a vaccine. I could not go to the pool. I could order all of my groceries delivered. I could stop living until I get the vaccine.

I am aware that the Covid19 virus has a very high survival rate for most people. I am aware that if I were not a sixty-seven year old type 1 diabetic that is statistically overweight and has medically controlled high blood pressure I could go about a fairly normal life and come though fine. When my factors are added into the equation it is more likely that if I get it I will have complications.

I do control my actions. I wear a mask whenever I leave my property. I even wear a mask in my car when I a driving although I am the only one in the car. I go out to swim. I shower in a private bathroom that has its own shower avoiding the locker room completely. I shop about once a week in off hours when I need things. Usually less than once a week.

The death toll for Covid19 in the United States surpassed the death toll for heart disease this week. Making it the number one killer in the United States during 2020. The thing is that many deaths from Covid19 are avoidable. Research has it that just wearing a mask could have prevented tens of thousands of deaths.

What is making my life feel complicated right now it that I am dependent on others caring about my health and my well-being to keep me healthy. Before this my actions could control virtually all situations.
