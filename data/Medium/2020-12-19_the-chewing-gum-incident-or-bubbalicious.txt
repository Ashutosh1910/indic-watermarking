The Chewing Gum Incident or Bubbalicious

Big Willie

Back in 2010 I worked on a miniseries with a bunch of big stars and I probably would have stayed on it as a daily AD but for another job opportunity. That week however did give me one of my favourite film stories/interactions with a celebrity.

It was Katie Holmes first day on the set and this was not long after her much publicized split with Tom Cruise. She had her daughter Suri with her in town and had brought her to set. I recall a play area was even set up for her. I remember it being a big deal and that we were to be on the lookout for paparazzi even though we were in somewhat of a remote location. There was apparently a ten thousand dollar bounty for getting a shot of mom & daughter.

Katie had a later call and eventually was brought to set by her two bodyguards. One a mammoth of a fellow who closely resembled Stone Cold Steve Austin. And the other, her driver was built more like a tank.

The set was busy and the Set PA asked me to meet and greet Ms. Holmes when she arrived. I had set up a bit of a green area prior to her arrival for her to sit outside if she wanted to. Just a director’s chair under a tree or something… Now I always err on the side of kid gloves when meeting a new actor, you’re never sure how they want or expect to be treated. When the Yukon pulled up she got out and turned on Suri, kissed her and said she’d see them back at the trailer (the babysitter, I’m guessing, was in the SUV as well). The six foot five bodyguard exited the far side and Katie turned to me.

“Hi Ms. Holmes” I said, then introduced myself. “Set is not quite ready for you yet but I’ve set up a spot for you to sit in the shade if you’d like or I can take you inside and introduce you to the team. She just looked at me and I guess noticed that I was chewing gum, she quietly asked “Do you have gum?”

“Uhh ya I do.” I stammered “But it’s been in my back pocket and I’ve been sitting on it…. I’d be happy to go to craft service and get you a fresh pack.” As I was saying this however she simply extended her arm and held out her hand. She almost cooed “Ass gum, my favourite.”

I had fished the pack of Dentyne Ice out of my sweaty cargo shorts I was wearing on that hot summer day and broke a piece out of the blister pack into her hand. Without any hesitation she popped it in her mouth and went “Mmmmmm.” All I could think was this chick is cool!

Then we went inside and I handed her off to the team and ended up with one of my favourite celebrity stories from my years in film.
