Luxury Dog

Alexander Nagel

He is on the bus to the shuttle that will take him to the airport at 7:30 in the morning. The haze between sleeping and waking elucidates a dream state wrought from the spectral anvils of heavy metal. He pushes his earbuds in deeper, moves his lower back forward, and stretches out his legs to allow his head enough space to lie back. Behind his eyelids, he is blind to the grey early morning misery of urban decay passing slowly by. Now he is free to take comfort in how long the battery of his phone will last while in airplane mode. He indulges in how unencumbered he is by heavy jacket or itchy cap and falls further into a dream. Calmed by the presence of his sneakers, T-shirt, jeans, and lack of luggage, his thoughts coalesce into a fragile decanter of stillness. Minutes dissolve into moments and moments transform into gentle sensations and soon he feels nothing save a satisfaction in the preservation of his battery.

Soon Linda enters his mind. He sees her smile and he wonders what she will look like in twenty years. They’ll live in an unassuming, yet comfortable house out in the desert somewhere. Far enough away that their neighbors won’t be able to hear them at night. Maybe they’ll have a dog. Maybe a cat. Maybe even some kids. She’ll want birds.

Permeating peace comes from the compartmentalizing of all their amenities into their proper place. Besides the necessary bedrooms, of which there will be many, and the kitchen, which will be scrubbed daily, he thinks that they both must have at least some space that they can call their own. She will undoubtedly have her studio. Perhaps it will be in a little Spanish style hacienda out back and not too far from the bougainvillea growing above the patio beside that single rotting saguaro. He would need some place that would be appropriate for his Merciful Fate posters and his drums. Maybe the garage…

Yes… the garage. A garage is a perfect place to have a few chairs with pleasantly worn cushions, a decent sound system, a television, refrigerator, dog bed, and not too many windows to keep out the bright daylight of the desert. It would be an ideal place for their dog.

A dog should always be kept outside the home, so as not to track filth into the house. But the garage was a different matter all together. He could take pride in constructing a place of warmth and safety where he would be able to give it all the attention it deserves. He would take all the time necessary to make sure his dog was properly trained, so they would know what’s right and what’s wrong. Dogs are only burdensome when they act out inappropriately. They only act out when their boundaries are not clearly defined.

He awakens suddenly to the squealing of hydraulic breaks. Without so much as a jacket, he is left waiting beneath a balmy overcast sky for a shuttle that is already late.
