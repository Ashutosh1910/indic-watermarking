E.N.T.E.R.T.A.I.N.M.E.N.T.

S.F.N. Cook

Would it be worth it?

Image public domain, courtesy of digitalcomicmuseum.com, modified from original.

Oono feared the anticipation, but got excited none-the-less to be on ENTERTAINMENT. ENTERTAINMENT was the future. ENTERTAINMENT was destiny. Eventually everyone would be on ENTERTAINMENT.

It was a virtual space where he and his click, a social group, could experience each other, inside and out, feelings and bodies. A reverberating fungus of emotions and shared experiences. His click had about sixty people now: Some people were scanned-in already and others, like him, were still outside-looking-in.

But that was changing today. This morning he had been scanned-in and now he was walking to his death. His other self, Oono 2.0 was there, on ENTERTAINMENT, with all of them now. Probably just waking up into that new life, that new experience, with all of those who had gone before him. They were ENTERTAINMENT, they made it up. Ice cubes floating in gelatin.

This part of the city was poor and dangerous. Graffiti and mud. It made him nauseous so he clicked-on for a quick dose of ENTERTAINMENT. The two worlds mixed like paint in a centrifuge. The warmth of ENTERTAINMENT spread over his scalp like butterscotch LSD. A hallucination-within-a-hologram with euphoric overtones. But he was only the carbon, not the scan. Oono 2.0 was the scan and was there now on ENTERTAINMENT.

It was a story of jealousy, to be the carbon, not the scan. He had lost consciousness when the scan started. He remembered hoping, naively, that he would somehow wake up to be the scan. But how could that have happened? He was the carbon and Oono 2.0 was the scan.

He was sitting in a studio. “Look up here,” the massive British brute said, gently winding the garrote around Oono’s neck but not putting pressure on it yet. Oono looked up into the man’s gray eyes. Someone with a camera shifted in his peripheral.

“Will it be worth it?” he asked the man with the garrote.

The man’s practiced focus shifted to fake sympathy: “Of course it is, mate! It’s ENTERTAINMENT! You’ve made it! You’re the #1 story today! Everyone’s watchin’ you!”

Oono looked at the camera and wished everyone from his click could be there with him. But all he saw was the confusing optical geometry of an articulating lens.

He had not be a rich man and could not pay money to get into ENTERTAINMENT. He had to pay with his death. A death that would be broadcast to all the clicks in the world. It was a big event to sell your death. Not many people had the guts to go through with it. Oono 2.0 would be there, sharing the experience of watching himself die. Oono 2.0 was somewhere out there watching the feed from the camera there at his side.

“Ready, mate?” the garrote asked.

Oono nodded and looked into his eyes. The big man put the pressure on Oono’s throat slowly. It was supposed to take a long time to die.
