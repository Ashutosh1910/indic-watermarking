2020's Memories

unknown

As I sit reflecting on my life on the eve of my 27th year being alive, this year has got to be my most successful and most trying year to date.

I feel I’ve grown. In fact I think I have grown so much, I get this sense of pride as if I am watching my child. I’ve grown in everything from career, to knowledge, strength, and even emotions. Calling it just a trail would be a huge understatement.

I was on a roll at the start of the year. I was knocking down goal one after another even well into the covid season. I felt on top of the world. Now at the end of the year I’m still reeling from one of the biggest depressive episodes I’ve ever had.

To be honest it is hard to believe so much has happened in the same year. I feel so different from who I was months ago.

I’ve matured nicely.

I’ve gone from strictly caring about being the best and following the plan, to believing and supporting the notion that there is nothing accomplishments or achievements can do to make you feel fulfilled. Instead we should find love in what we do, and also in the person who is doing it.

I’ve gone from emotionally detached from the world, to someone who can empathize with others and shed tears for even those I don’t know.

I’ve gone from being avoidant of attachments, to someone who has experience visceral emotions only two kindred souls passing one another could experience.

I’ve gone from someone who wore a mask everyday, admiring and wishing I could be as brave as those who walk freely without one, to a person who still wears his mask but from time to time I take it off and be myself.

I’ve matured nicely.

I feel like my entire life I’ve been living reserved from being myself. This year I learned how much I hated that. I decided to be me again after all this time. It feels like I am in a new body learning to walk again. Its tuff. All I’ve been doing is stumbling. I would be lying if I said I even knew who I am. All I have is a feeling and a nagging voice inside my head.

It demands that I live a genuine life I can be proud of.

Unfortunately that means saying no to the things I think I want in order to not forsake the things my soul needs. That means stepping out of line and going the opposite direction of the world at times. All because everything in my being calls the world wrong even if I am the only one.

It’s a conflicting feeling cause I feel as if I am getting in my own way at times. Like I am stuck between a rock and a hard place. I have to be honest with my myself and that is one of the heaviest chains you can carry.

I feel lost and confused.

So many questions. How is it that I have aged once again but I feel like a child still. I am frustrated and I can’t explain why. Its actually pretty humorous to admit.

I guess all this is to say I am evolving (I think?) and I’ll see you guys on the other side of 2020.
