About Me- Anna Dainese

Anna Dainese

I was born and live in Italy, I love what this country has to offer, I love the people who are here, their warmth, I love our food (who doesn't?). Italy is a country rich in culture and art, things that I love, in fact a few years ago I graduated in History of Art.

But like any self-respecting twenty-year-old I lately found myself in the midst of an existential crisis and I began to understand that perhaps art was just a passion for me, a great stimulus, but it was not destined to be my future.

Slowly then I approached the world of writing, a world that has involved me as long as I can remember. For example, I remember an episode when I was in elementary school, as a task the teacher had assigned us to write a theme in which we had to describe a relative of ours. I chose my cousin, I don't know why, we don't even have who those great relationship, but I chose her. I vividly remember this sentence:

My cousin has very light eyes and golden hair, but the most characteristic thing about her face are her ears, ah her ears ... etc

The teacher correcting it eliminated "ah her ears" because she had understood it as a repetition. Well it HAD to be a repetition, its purpose was just to emphasize the phrase and the beauty of the ears but she didn't understand it. And that was the first time (but not the last) that a teacher didn't understand how deep I could be. Perhaps the fault was in my shyness and insecurity, but none of my teachers have ever tried to understand what was under that barrier.

But I kept writing, and then one day I found my first and most important supporter, my husband.

He made me discover Medium, he helped me to understand how to move within it and it is always him who always supports and encourages me (most of the applause for my articles is his). I met him eight years ago, he is my brother's best friend, but I wrote a message to him to declare myself almost five years ago. The rest is history, a beautiful story, but it would take a book to tell it.

So that I love art, writing and food I already said it. Another important thing to say however is the passion for my children, I might be biased but they are the best.

The first one was born when I was very young, and therefore I would say that in part becoming a mother shaped me, it shaped my adult life. But I don't like to present myself as a mother. I find it limiting and even a little reductive. Partly because when people hear the word mom they imagine a desperate housewife who spends her days in sweatsuit in a minivan to carry her children in their infinity commitments, and partly because being a mother is not all that defines me.

I am also that but above all I am much more. I am a dreamer, my mental films win an Academy Awards every year. I'm an avid reader, my favorite book so far is Blindness of Saramago. I'm a good cook, and an excellent taster (my belly speaks for me).
