A Beast’s Farewell

R. B. Sheehan

Why am I doing this? she asked herself with an icy sigh. Does he even deserve it? Her numb fingers fought to maintain their waning grasp on the frozen rope as it writhed for freedom. She realized she should’ve questioned this task long before it began.

Braving winter’s wind atop a ten-story parking garage, she stood in wait, wondering, Is it too late to back out? Then without warning, her heart lurched at the sound of his disembodied voice piercing the twilight fog.

From his hazy end of the noose, he begged her, “Please! Whatever you do, don’t let go.”

She never wanted to let go of something more in her life. “I won’t,” she replied with not-so-subtle hesitance. She hadn’t expected to be in that position, but there she was, far too involved to escape. What will people think of me? Qualms continued to creep through her mind as her toes turned to ice. What will my parents say when they find out what I’ve done?

He, too, was struggling. His troubles weren’t with opinions or freezing temperatures but with his desire for happiness, for love, for life. He was unable to recall the last time he felt anything of the sort. Ages of fighting this solitary beast on his own left him desperate; he couldn’t take it any longer.

He finally found someone, skeptical but willing, and brought her here. He needed help with his demon, as it was beyond his strength to conquer alone. With no remorse for his neglected responsibilities, he finished tying his knot.

I’m just happy she agreed to this, he mused as he wrestled with the fiend once more. After all his suffering, after months without hope, he knew the time had come. “Okay, I’m ready!” he shouted. “Are you ready?”

Though reeling with disbelief, deep down, she began to feel the pull of a shameful pleasure. Her fear of a new thrill faded. Fuck it, she thought, silencing the last of her embarrassment. “Do it!” she demanded, electric with anticipation.

Her words, sailing on a serendipitous gust, pushed him to release the monster. Time slowed as his eyes rose, watching the rope, the beast, the pain drift out of sight. This was his idea, his choice, his responsibility. In that moment, he regretted nothing. His misery was at an end.

With a sudden immunity to the cold and a titan’s grip on the cord, she gasped at the scene unfolding before her eyes. A slight smile emerged on her face. Why am I enjoying this? Should I feel guilty? she wondered as she felt no guilt whatsoever.

Minutes passed. She hadn’t moved, stunned in the light of her newfound joy.

“Well, was it everything you hoped for?” he teased, now standing at her side, wearing the widest grin he’s worn in years.

“It’s better than I expected!” She let out a light laugh. “I’m loving the big dragon! So cool,” she confessed as she turned to him. Her honest smile spoke the truth of her heart. I never thought I’d fall in love with an extreme kiting enthusiast.

They marveled together as the tethered beast floated through the fog.
