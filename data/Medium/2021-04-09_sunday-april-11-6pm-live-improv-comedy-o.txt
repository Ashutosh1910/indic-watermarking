Sunday April 11 6pm LIVE Improv Comedy off-Broadway Times Square NYC

Walt Frasier

After a year of ZOOM class and shows, the cast of EIGHT IS NEVER ENOUGH Improv is excited to return Off Broadway, Times Square NYC. Saturdays April 10 at 3pm will be the troupes first public show since March 12, 2020. The show will include skits and songs improvised based on audience suggestions. A number of games require more audience participation, however – at least for this first weekend – we will not be inviting audience on stage. The show’s climatic finish is a mini Broadway Musical improvised form a single word. Click Here for discount tickets.

YOUR SAFETY and the safety of our cast, crew and staff is our biggest concern.

Seating will occur 2:45-3pm. No one arriving after 3:05 will be allowed into the theater. Groups of up to 6 may sit together. All groups will be separated from other groups by a safe distance. Temperature checks and contact tracing info will be collected at the theater entrance. Masks must be worn when not eating/drinking.
