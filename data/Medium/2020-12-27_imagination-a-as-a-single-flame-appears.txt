Imagination — A. As a single flame appears from thin…

C.A. Martinez

As a single flame appears from thin air, and the blackness of absence begins to melt into its warmth, what was once nothing is, suddenly, something. But how can nothingness — the absence of matter, the absence of absence — spawn a tangible, physical mass of energy? Like a thought that pops into our mind? Like a contemplation that sails atop the waves of our brains, those tempestuous seas of which we live within and never truly learn to navigate? What a conundrum, and what an answer to that preposterous apparition; the sudden appearance of an issue, a question, a puzzle, a hex! Through the folds, the hills and valleys of what drives us forward, we spawn countless ideas and thus, countless obstacles. Those obstacles, when arranged in certain patterns, then become acts of genius, works of art, intuitive stupidity, and innovative regularity. That has been thought of before, in the vast ocean of another, and yet he who withholds the incarnation from his people takes no credit. That is reality, is it not? The one who opens his jowls and propels his throat — five seconds, five minutes, five millennia later — he is the king of that ideation, but in the technicality of what is more than likely the truth, he is a fraud, for his ancestor precipitated that little flame from the nothingness of thought and thus, holds the title of that truth.

And so, is that the universe? Is it the fraud of another’s incantation? The literature of that pure genius now plagiarized by Space and Time? We kneel before them and base this existence upon the rules we perceive of them, even as they work and play differently depending on such a simple premise as perspective. We are toyed with! Take the puppet master that is your brain; imagine! You could display within your mind’s eye your greatest enemy, your greatest fears, your greatest desires; with these inner concepts you may do as you please. That enemy you may strike down yourself, with godlike power; you may see him ravaged by wolves, begging for the mercy only you could bestow; there could be a narrative in which he travels through volumes and volumes of tragedy after tragedy, at the clemency of your pen. No, it is not visible to the rest of the world, with their eyes or minds, but it is a visceral augury in which you can live, and it will activate your brain in the same ways as if you truly bore the leash to your enemy’s collar! And is that not real then? To affect you in such clandestine authenticity? I am God almighty within the confines of the same organ in which I perceive the Earth; how can it be that what is imagined is a fallacy? It is no more a fallacy than witnessing the impossible — the spacemen staring at our great blue marble, laden with men who swear upon their souls that it is a flat disc — what an imagination! Then, to the grave these disc-men go, to the graves, in their chest do they believe in their escapism, and to the graves go others with the forethought that divine judgment awaits them.
