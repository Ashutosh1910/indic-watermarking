Hummus and Prosecco (…gets you through a virtual Christmas party)

Claire Diot-Lefebvre

A letter to the Head of Human Resources

Paris, 18th of December 2020

Dear Ms. X, Head of Human Resources,

Honestly, I have to admit it, it must be hard to work in the Human Resources Department right now… How are you supposed to build a team spirit in the company, when the Christmas party has just gone bust??… One of these rare events where everyone forgets about resentment and anger to focus on the Christmas buffet ?

Yes, it must be frustrating that this high of the year, that brings everyone together in the same joy, holiday cheer and drunkenness, gets cancelled, but not annual reviews, payroll, timesheets or holiday approvals…

So you came up with this crazy idea… Why not put a virtual Christmas party together?

You sent us all an email with a Zoom link for the wildest event of the year (I mean, wildest event at the office obviously…). You instructed us to come up with an idea for a virtual Secret Santa present… And you even sent us a box full of treats by post. Working for a French company is nice: I got lots of crackers, a mini tin of foie gras, black olive mash (tapenade), rosemary butter cookies and a bottle of champagne… Thanks!

OK, I have to admit, I got it two days in advance and ate it all before the day of the party. As I still wanted to have something to eat that evening, I just made a quick hummus. I emptied a tin of chickpeas in my food processor, added a garlic clove (I would usually not add a whole clove to avoid bad breath, but COVID was on my side: I was by myself and I could go wild…), the juice of half a lemon, two tablespoons of Tahini, two tablespoons of olive oil, a pinch of cumin, salt and pepper. I pulsed until smooth, adding water as needed. I also added fresh coriander and gave a few more pulses to mix it in.

Then I gathered (with myself) in front of my laptop and joined 28 of my colleagues (I guess Christmas zoom parties are not possible in bigger companies)?… Obviously I was ready to open a bottle of Prosecco. Nobody would ever notice it wasn’t the company’s champagne, which I had drunk the night before…

As expected, everything was a bit awkward : We were looking at each other like fish look at bystanders on the other side of their aquarium walls; our boss made a speech and cracked a few jokes to keep everyone’s attention (and to spot who was not listening…); you praised us for keeping up the good work despite the distance. Then we all opened our bottle of (whatever it was) and cheered virtually.

But you know what? Despite the awkwardness of this all, it was nice… Maybe it was the hummus. Maybe the Prosecco. Maybe the fact that Andrew, whom I have been flirting with since the start of the lockdown, sent me some jokes via the private chat. Or just maybe a mix of it all… Anyway, I just wanted to ask you… Do you happen to know who my Secret Santa was? As I got a YouTube video of “All I want for Christmas is you” by Mariah Carey, and I was hoping it may have come from Andrew?

Thanks a lot in advance,
