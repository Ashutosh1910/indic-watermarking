Journaling

Deepika Singh

Writing an actual diary vs writing an electronic journal.

I had an epiphany once that I was able to process my thoughts better and go with the flow and not bother about small things during the day. And the only thing that I was doing that time was keeping a diary for myself, writing daily how things went for me, what made me feel something, the thoughts I had in my mind. Like writing an actual diary, not an electronic one. One more thing I was doing, as I was coming across a lot of things I wanted to find more about those things later on. So I kept writing about those topics in a page in the diary. Keeping it for later access when free time and thinking I will pick one thing at a time in the future and then just get to know about them and then move to the next thing. And the thing is, I did start doing it. I picked up one thing and then the other, but then some things were really broad to find out about and so, I just gave up on them, because of the time crunch. And then I couldn’t time for myself during the day to sit and go over those things. There was always something or the other coming up. And now the thing is those things that I wanted to know about are just sitting on the diary because right now I don’t know when to sit and explore on those things.

The main concern in this situation is my diary is a hard copy. One day I am just gonna keep it away somewhere, where I won’t access it too much. And then one day I will just stop using it. And those things I wrote about they are just gonna go.

Wowww. I just figured out the solution to the problem I am facing by writing it out in this journal. Man. This is good.

Solution:

When writing a diary, write into an actual journal where you can pour your heart out and can just go with the flow. You have the privacy for yourself. When writing on an electronic medium you will be connected to the internet and thus you are never disconnected with the outside rush and cannot have that alone time for yourself. By dedicatedly writing into an actual journal we have that time for ourselves, where we can let ourselves and our thoughts flow freely and that way we discover things about ourselves which we wouldn’t have otherwise.

But then there is also this thing that you may not always have a pen and a paper handy but you always have your phone. A medium acting as the pen and paper for most people. And of you fail to find a pen and a paper near by, you always have the option to turn to your phone for the self unleash.

And for the things that need our attention again and again, like a list that I need to look again and find more about some things, we can write them into an electronic medium where they will be preserved for a longer time. And we can refer to that list whenever we want in the future also.

Woww. Problem solved. Thanks to journaling!!
