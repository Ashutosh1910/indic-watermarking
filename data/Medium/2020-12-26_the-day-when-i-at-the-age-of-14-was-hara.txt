The day when I, at the age of 14, was harassed while in the cinema alone

Juliano Righetto

This man seemed to choose very well where he would sit. He went to the front row and sat away from the three men. Okay… I kept watching the movie. After a few minutes, the man in red stood up and sat next to one of the three other men in the front row this time. A few minutes passed… And this man got up and left. I noticed that.

The man in red immediately looked for another “target.” Within minutes he had already sat down next to another of the men in the front row. Seconds later, this man got up and left.

I was watching… And I was worried. The movie had barely started; I knew it would be a long time before I left. And there were only four more men before he reached me.

It is easy to predict that the behavior continued. He sat next to the third man, he left. He looked back. He saw the two men in the second row. He did the same thing — he sat down next to them — and the two repeated the behavior — they left.

I started to sweat cold…

He looked back…

And he came to sit beside the man who was in my row, but still far from me. A while passed… And the man also left.

I was staring at the screen in desperation for the dolphin scene to start, I wanted to know everything that happened in the film, but I had no idea what that older man wanted with me!

The man in red sat down on a seat next to me.

Nothing about the dolphin scene…

He changed his seat and sat by my side.

Where the fuck are the dolphins, damn it?

At that moment, the cinema door opened. An intense light came in from outside; everyone looked back. I imagine that one of the men who left had been complaining to the security guards… I thought I was saved…

But it was just someone late. The person just sat in one of the armchairs and watched the movie.

And nothing about the dolphins…

The man in red started talking to me:

OLD MAN IN RED — (REFERRING TO ROY SCHEIDER) This guy over there was in “Jaws,” right?

ME — (NERVOUS) Yes…

OLD MAN IN RED — Does it bother you if I sit next to you?

ME — (SHY AND POLITE) No… But I’m already leaving…

AND WHERE THE HELL ARE THOSE DOLPHINS???

It was then that I finally understood what made the other men leave… the older man in red took his leather folder and started to put it on my lap! He wanted, I imagine, to masturbate me! I reacted, of course:

ME — Hey, what is that?

OLD MAN IN RED — Ah, sorry… Does it bother you?

ME — Yes, of course, it does.

BUT I DID NOT GO AWAY… After all, I wanted to see the dolphin scene! The man in red tried to put the folder on my lap again, I fought, he picked it up… And he tried once more. Luckily and finally…
