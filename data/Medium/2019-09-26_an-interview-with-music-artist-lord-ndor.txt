Biography:

Michael Ndoro better knows by his stage name “LordNdoro” is an Australian rapper, Producer, from Darwen Northern Territory. Born in Bulawayo, Zimbabwe in 1998 he immigrated to Australia at the tender age of 6. Filled with a passion for music from a young age, he started pursuing professionally in 2017.

Lord Ndoro’s music flows from him as a natural emotional response to what is going on in his life at the time. Each track has its own personality and connection to his state of mind. Passionately he delivers a unique sound with each release, which surprises and delights.

Q: Your new single ‘Take A Look Aroundʼ is out Who inspired you. Can you talk us through that?

A: well the track was mainly inspired by two girls I used to date a while back but ended up going our separate ways due to me wanting to focus more on my music and just some other personal reasons from both sides.

Q: How did you come up with the title?

A: it was pretty much the first few words I heard from the chorus when the beat got sent to me so I just thought it would be much easier and simple for people to remember the track if I just used the main words in the chorus as the title.

Q: What was the recording process for ‘Take A Look Around?

A: it was actually pretty quick like as soon as I heard the beat I went straight to my studio and just started writing the first thing that came to my mind. but the second verse was a bit difficult because I was still in good terms with my ex but at the same time, I still wanted to flex on her with my new/current girl so yeah it took some time to convince my self that it was the right move to make for the track.

Q: Do you think your exʼs have heard the song yet? and if so how do u think they felt about the second verse or the whole song?

A: yeah I can definitely confirm that they have because one of them blocked me straight after I sent them the track with the caption “I made the second verse especially for you ;)” lol and to be honest I could care less how they fell about the song “hahah”

Q: What artists are inspiring you at the moment?

A: right now I would have to say Travis Scott and Russ (Travis Scott just for the fact that his AstroWorld documentary has made me want to focus more on music and just explore different sounds) and with Russ, I feel that his consistency with track releases just inspires me to push harder.

Q: You have collaborated with some impressive artists over the last few years — who is on your dream list of people to work with?

A: I would have to say #1 Joey Bada$$ #2 Russ #3 Post Malone #4 21 Savage and last but not least Travis Scott & The Weeknd.

Q: What do you think the music scene in darwin Is missing that other states arenʼt?

A: well to be honest I think itʼs like the lack of communication through artists and just like getting together and supporting each other as a whole instead of everyone going their own direction and trying to do their own thing (which is not a bad thing but — — like there has to be a point In between where we all meet as a whole and just feed each other ideas and help each other where itʼs needed).

Q: Whatʼs up next for Lord Ndoro?

A: well Iʼm currently just working on my album for next year and some tracks Iʼm planing releasing before the end of this year
