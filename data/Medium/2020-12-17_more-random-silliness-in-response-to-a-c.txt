Saying goodbye to 2020 and cheers to 2021, it can’t come fast enough. Photo take from author’s library.

Yep, life in 2020 has been a rollercoaster, metaphorically speaking because, of course, we didn’t go to any amusement parks; they were all closed. In fact we didn’t go hardly anywhere. So some of my favorite memories came in the form of texts and pictures on my phone.

Grandchildren

Due to all the restriction, I rarely visit with my granddaughters in person. The couple times we did meet in person, masks are adorned while I walk around with spray Lysol and a pillowcase. Pillowcase? You ask. When Kinsley really, really needed a hug, she climbed in a pillowcase and we cuddled.

Traveling in a self-contained camp trailer

My husband and I were lucky enough to travel a little in our 23 foot trailer. Even during a pandemic, this was possible because we had no contact with other forms of life. Well, except for the three skunks that took up residency under our home on wheels. No, I didn’t get a picture.

Since we didn’t visit any major points of interest, I found myself taking pictures of signs that caught my interest.
