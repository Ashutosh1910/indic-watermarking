I Just Want To See You Grow

𝘋𝘪𝘢𝘯𝘢 𝘊.

To whomever needs this message,

I have been feeling drawn to write these words for a while now. I don’t know why and I don’t even know whether it’s me who needs to read them or somebody else. I say we shouldn’t get too concerned with the “whys” but rather with how we can transmit the message to the world. In truth, it will always only reach those in need, whether they’re consciously aware of it or not.

Pain is the catalyst for growth. Despite our efforts to avoid it, numb it, or rush to overcome it without processing it first, pain will continuously follow us around, like a loyal friend. Take note, I said friend…Not enemy. Why is that?

An enemy doesn’t want to see you evolve and succeed. An enemy’s attention is directed towards failure and disappointment. It will use all the tools available to it to make you fall into temptations that are highly rewarding in the short term, but sabotaging and destructive in the long term.

Pain is on the opposite side of the coin. All its efforts are directed towards your journey of discovery and growth. It doesn’t have any needs, other than seeing you embrace it. It doesn’t want to watch you hurting and every time it senses that you’re struggling with resistance, it sends out signals which manifest in the energy field first, and then in the physical body. Sometimes these signals manifest as tiredness, as a result of energy leakage. Other times as illnesses. The seriousness of these manifestations is directly proportional to the amount of time and effort spent avoiding pain.

Yesterday I had my first panic attack in over two years. It was out of the blue like all panic attacks are. It was scary because I forgot how to deal with this situation. I struggled to breathe through the mask, which I believe made me feel even more nauseous. It lasted long enough to raise a red flag and make me reflect on the possible root cause.

A panic attack is mild compared to what other people may experience. However, it's not something that should get dismissed. I now know it was a signal that stems from unprocessed emotional hurts. I know it took a lot of avoidance to get to this point again. I know it’s serious and I am already shifting my focus internally and giving myself time to process and heal.

To heal. That’s all our lovely friend, pain, requires us to do. Of course, healing is an ongoing process, a journey rather than a destination. It’s not like you heal once and job is done. No, no. That’s too easy and comfortable. That’s more of an “enemy’s territory.”

With pain, things don’t necessarily go as smoothly as that. They are not comfortable, easy, and straightforward. They require a commitment to growth and resilience. As that saying goes “God never gives you more than you can handle.”

Well, pain never gives you more than you can handle either. Take this as a direct message from the Universe that you’re going to make it trough this cycle of struggles you’re experiencing. You are growing and evolving beautifully! You are.
