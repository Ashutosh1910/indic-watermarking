If you have no idea what I’m talking about, read this: This is my 2020 Diary and it’s going to get messy (I have cancer).

Back to this radiation thing: I go for treatment five days per week, Monday through Friday. The whole appointment takes about 30 minutes each time.

This is what’s up:

Ya girl is tired, but I’m not exhausted. I think it’s more mental and emotional than anything else at the moment.

My skin is already getting super dry and unpleasant in the areas I’m being treated. Like, ew.

I’m more stupid thirsty all the time than I normally am. What’s that about?

I’ve had a great hair day every day this week. Shout out to low humidity for making that happen.

See? Told ya.

I sent this dumb picture to my family earlier in the week because, as I said, I’ve been having some good hair days. It’s always worth sharing with folks when you’re having a good day (especially if you have a little sister; gotta rub it in when you can).

Having all those good hair days got me thinking about the other good things I like sharing with people, which led to me making a list over the course of the week so I wouldn’t forget. Here’s what I came up with.

My big list of good things worth sharing with people:

Laughter

Postcards

Your dreams

Good hair days

Mediterranean food

Your bottle of Sprite

Selfies at the Grand Canyon

Road trips across the country

Road trips through the desert

Homemade chocolate chip cookies

Sitting on the porch watching the sunset/sunrise

Music that makes you feel that feeling in your heart and your stomach and your feet and your face

That crossword puzzle you finally finished; or that thing you colored; or that flower you planted; or whatever it is you’re super proud of today

Now, I’m fully aware that this list isn’t actually all that big. It looked a lot bigger on the sticky note I was using, okay?

What I want now, no, what I need is for you to add to it.

I need you to share some good with me so I can tuck it away in my pocket and pull it out when I need it. This isn’t just for radiation treatment.

This is for any rough day in general that either of us is having. In the moment, when your hair sucks, or your day sucks, or you’re frustrated, or you’re sad, of you’re feeling lonely, it can be hard to come up with “good things” on the spot.

I’m just trying to cook up a good moment for future me and you if you’re down. This is my thing. You just have the opportunity to be part of it.

Help me build this list. Help me build it for both of us.

But only do it if you mean it.
