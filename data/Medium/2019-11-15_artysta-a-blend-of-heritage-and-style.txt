Here is a synopsis of these art forms-:

Warli Art-:

Warli art is a style of rural Indian tribal art that originates from the Maharashtra state and presents scenes from their daily life such as harvest, weddings, festivals, cultures etc. It is centered around the concept of nature, so components of nature are central points in these Warli paintings.

Dhokra Art-:

Dhokra art is grown up from the tribes of West Bengal. In this art, metal figurines of human and animals are the main feature points that give traditional touch.

Madhubani Art-:

This art is a colorful characterization of culture, heritage, beliefs, and rituals of the Mithila region of Bihar. The most common themes like God and Goddess, Sun and Moon and geometric shapes are used in Madhubani art.
