Futurism destroyed humanity

Filip Djukic

World authorities have been training people for a year and teaching them new behaviors. Behavior that requires sterility, alienation as well as keeping distance in physical and spiritual form.

Alienation comes in every form, so studies showing (https://www.liebertpub.com/doi/abs/10.1089/cyber.2016.0201) how social networks separate people from each other, not connect them, have taken over every subject of human interaction in the most unhealthy way possible.

The removal of everyday human interaction that is currently happening will cause a man to enter various paradigms. The human species, Homo sapiens sapiens, will lose touch with a form of behavior that was normal thirty years ago.

Industry and the development in the early 20th century brought new patterns of behavior and destroyed some old ones.

That is happening now. This futurism and rapid digitalization have entered another pore of human existence.

Real-time communication and information that can be transmitted the fastest so far can confuse everyone as one information can undo the next.

The confusion of information that cannot be absorbed creates general chaos in understanding what is correct and what’s not.

Although the mentioned industrial revolution brought about structural and core changes in the human psyche and understanding of the world, this digitalization will take away much more.

It should not sound futuristic that in twenty years from now, people will have built-in chips with which they will be able to regulate and solve current affairs.

The new man will have a better memory (when he installs microchips that will help him in this) will be less ill, eugenics will also get involved in creating a ‘better’ man, and virtuality will become a new reality.

No, these are not some assumptions just because twenty years ere it was unthinkable to have a cell phone that could answer almost all our possible questions, bring us to the exact location, and more.

The notion of a cell phone with which we can also share our thoughts, videos, pictures, and more was equally incomprehensible futurism.

I don’t think the man is ready to receive that much advancement in technology. And humans probably never be able for it. The French philosopher Jean Baudrillard observed that man had the only reality in the Middle Ages when he had to go a certain way on foot or with a horse and produce food with his own hands.

Coronavirus and this time we are in now will teach us new patterns of behavior and will prepare new generations in advance for the new world.

It is already strange to shake hands, approach someone close, hug someone, and if you forget the mask in the apartment, you know that you cannot enter anywhere without it.

This current situation will change, the condition before Coronavirus will return, but the consequences will remain.

After every huge event, enormous changes happen. From Sept. 11 to Kennedy’s assassination. We can only imagine how many changes will follow after a coronavirus pandemic that lasts a whole year.

It’s not even crucial what one wants to achieve by vaccination (if anything ), just as it does not matter that people are currently in various blockages, it is essential that one wants to make a change in human behavior and psyche.

The new crisis, which will further destroy rural areas and drive people into the city, will network people in new virtual networks where large companies can control the market and influence human needs.

As it is already possible to influence us, offer us certain products that might interest us, new networking of people into an even greater virtual network — will allow companies to create new “needs” for us. We will all be connected to one system so it will be easier to create supply and demand.

In translation, large companies will be able to instill in us the awareness of what we need.

If you think this is not likely, take a look at this world now. They managed to instill in us the awareness that human contact is not good because it affects our health. Playing the card of guilt always works, that you can take someone’s life because you interacted with them is an even better strategy.

They can convince us that we have new needs that we didn’t have before.

And that is the scariest part.
