The following is a fictitious short-story written by me for you. Hope you’ll like it! Enjoy.

Name of story / Title - THE USUAL UNUSUAL :

On the first day of the month of love, Kabir while getting ready for the college, first kept aside but later wore his red sweatshirt, which was gifted to him by Gauri when she had proposed him on the Valentine’s day of last year.

Today, that is 1st of February, the entire class has already pre-planned and decided to come in red color clothes to the college, simply for fun and unofficially inaugurate the month of love.

In the afternoon after attending all the lectures, other students went to their regular places to chill and hangout and Kabir as usual made his way to the college library. To his surprise he saw that including his favorite first row 1st chair, other all chairs were also occupied and the complete library was full of people. Fortunately right at that moment he noticed the people of the second row were packing-up their bags and they were about to leave the library, so Kabir immediately went their and eventually grabbed the second row 2nd chair. After being indulge in his reading for more than an hour or so and side-by-side taking notes , he instantly realized both the two adjacent chairs, on Kabir’s left and right were no more empty now. A guy and a girl had settled themselves & within the next two minutes, Kabir found himself observing a argument which was taking place between them, where one was demanding to switch off the fan above their row and the other for keeping it onn, which was later was followed by an unusual stares to each other & simultaneously highlighting one another’s choices & habits in front of the entire library audience. And all of these together made Kabir easily understood that this couple might just had a fight or a small dispute which is also why they opted to sit beside him as only the 1st and 2nd chair of the 3rd row were empty. In the next very moment the girl was scribbling angrily on her notebook pages while cursing the male gender behavior loudly, she was clearly saying to made the boy hear it and he did so. It also irked the guy, & in reply he started justifying himself by facing towards her and speaking even more loudly so much so that the librarian even warned him twice. All of these was enough to make Kabir firmly believe that surely theirs been a fight between this couple. On the spur of the moment Kabir discovered that his favorite first row 1st chair was now empty and therefore Kabir was ready to make a move, leaving this couple alone and thinking that possibly they will then talk and end their fight or at least begin to do so. In addition to this, before leaving he hurriedly plus cleverly hiding from two of them, Kabir wrote some words on a piece of paper and gave one-one to both of them one-by-one, making the gesture that the person sitting next to him has passed it for the boy and for the girl respectively. He silently then stood-up and then acquired the first row 1st chair. The boy and the girl after reading that chit of paper smilingly looked at each other and the boy embarked to the chair between them on which Kabir was sitting earlier. The girl then slowly moved her hand over his hand !

Those 2 notes written by Kabir said the following lines-

(1.) I’m sorry, you know I’m an idiot, Please forgive me.

-Love, Krishna.

(2.) I’m sorry, you know I’m an idiot, Please forgive me.
