Cracking The Code- Review

To be very honest it is my first book which i had ordered it from amazon for myself and for my interest. Wait here, i am not going to give you spoiler about the book. As i am writing my first blog so i just decided to review the book- CRACKING THE CODE.

Reading the first page of the book i got the idea that this will be the perfect for me, at least for those who have some sort of interests in Bollywood or have dream of going to Bollywood.

CRACKING THE CODE- by Ayushmann Khuranna , which includes about his past struggles, some dark sides of Bollywood and reality shows and some powerful codes which helped him to crack all these barriers.

Post reading of this book what i have learnt that nothing is easy as it seems to be. And according to my opinion,in reference to Bollywood this book will be the best example of what that ishaan, a little boy told in TAARE ZAMEEN PAR that ‘Jo Dikhta hai wo hota nahi, aur jo hota hai wo dikhta nahi ’ Which simply means that having the best acting skills and good dramatic expressions will not be enough to be a good actor, if you want to see yourself in B-TOWN then apart from these things you should also have patience. The most important which i learned while reading that never reject any opportunity because it will definitely help you reach to your destination and that what he written in his Code#8- Give your best to whatever opportunity you get; never underestimate the significance of even the smallest of junctures. Your past actions will one day become your future redemption. The most irony part comes where he exposed the truth of reality shows that reality shows are no more real behind the camera. From selecting the contestants for the shows till deciding the winner of the show, unfortunately everything is scripted just for the sake of TRP and that is the bitter truth that the one who is capable of winning the show gets eliminated. Being as a host of that show Ayushmann learned the most things out there. And what i feel that is why nowadays there are 5–6 shows produced on every other channel which basically are on the same topic. Talking to the other code of the book where he told to his readers that always make your critics a best friend and never eliminate them or block them because they will basically act as a catalyst and be the reason of your success. Coming to the difference of personal and professional life. It is always important to maintain a gap between these two things and if you are able to balance between them then you are the successful and the happy one in your life.

So keeping with a promise which i made at the starting of this blog that i will not give you spoiler of this book so now i am not reviewing too much about this book. I will just recommend you that if you are also a dreamer of seeing yourself in Bollywood in next 10–20 years then this will be one for you. Here ending my blog by writing few of my favorite lines from the book CRACKING THE CODE- The gates to learning open only when the route to self-obsession is blocked.
