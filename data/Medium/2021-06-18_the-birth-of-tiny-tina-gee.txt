The Birth of Tiny Tina Gee

Lara Marks

My love of shadow dancing through trippy visuals started back in 2008 when a boyfriend gifted me his unwanted projector. I’m not sure where the idea came from originally but I found myself hosting preparties before gigs where I’d project the iTunes visualizer on the wall. My friends and I would dance through the colourful shapes with joy. This was accompanied sometimes with glow bracelets and always with cheap vodka. I loved the projector and would play my favourite music videos of the time including a remix of Yelle’s A Cause des Garcons, a tektonic dance masterpiece.

A few years back, both my parents and my brother threw in to get me a moderately fancy projector, a new one. The OG projector never made it to Australia but I always missed her. My new one arrived and indeed it was fancy. I lived in sharehouses so it was mainly used to watch movies and tv series. Depending on the house, it was used semi-regularly but sometimes hid under the bed for months.

Fast forward to March 2020: I had been working in events and entertainment and was forced to take leave without pay indefinitely. As a temporary visa holder in Australia, I received no government support. At all. I was also unable to seek out alternative work as I was only legally in Australia on an employer-sponsored work visa. A rock and a hard place you see. The point is I no longer had an income. However, I had a shit ton of free time on my hands.

I started projecting trippy visuals on the wall after dark. I chose whatever I could find on Youtube that looked appealing and I began to dance. I made playlists and I started playing around with ways to record myself. After some trial and error, I found a way. And the videos were pretty cool. I played music from my shitty speaker and recorded videos with my iPhone. As the pandemic pressed on, I was making videos every night.

During this time and since I wasn’t working, I was always looking for ways to save money. One way was scamming free food through through Hello Fresh. I found a way to screw the system and acquired multiple free food boxes. They have a first time trial offer, which I used with my name and address initially and then I started making up names and using neighbour’s addresses. There were quite a few but my favourite fictional persona was Tina Guarez from Apartment 1. Her email was tinytinagee@gmail and I just loved the idea of this imaginary petite Latina living downstairs.

Months go by and I continued dancing every night and making videos. I decided I make an online portfolio and picked Instagram as the platform. @tinytinagee was born.
