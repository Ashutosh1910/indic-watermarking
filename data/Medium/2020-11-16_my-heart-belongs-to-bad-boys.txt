My Heart Belongs to Bad Boys

Ms. Part Time Wino

Hey! Are you a Bad Boy? Because if so, I want to date you.

I don’t care if you’ve dropped out of high school, can’t hold down a job, or even have your driver’s license. As long as you 1) play in a band and 2) have more than three tattoos, then baby — I’m already yours.

(Also, major bonus points if you are the brooding lead singer.)

I don’t know why, but I can’t help myself around Bad Boys like yourself. Like a hormone-driven moth to a flame, I seek out men who are filled with passion and angst and all that other emotional drama I lack in my boring, middle-class upbringing.

And did I mention that my parents might hate you? Because they absolutely will. They’ll stare at the leather jacket you carelessly throw on their couch like the dead body of a homeless man you just dragged in and left for them to clean up.

“But we raised such a polite young woman. How could this happen?” they’ll bawl as they watch us pull out of their driveway.

(In my car, obviously, because you don’t have your license).

Even if you make me cry, contemplate my self-worth, and get overly jealous over small things like looking at the waitress for one second too long, being with you is oh so exciting.

For example, I love how you come with an unlimited supply of baggage. I know how rough it was for you growing up in the city (something my suburban pea-size brain simply can’t comprehend). Your dad was an alcoholic, and your mom had too many single guy friends floating around the house. Baby, you’ve been hurt badly.

And that’s why I let you treat me like shit.

You’re not an asshole. You’re just misunderstood. And that makes you weary of being in a relationship.

“I’m just not worthy of love,” you snarl while secretly texting that hot girl you met at the bar who you disguised in your phone as Dave Motorcycle Repair Man.

Believe me, no one understands the pain you’re going through like I do. When you fail to text me back or show up for our “hanging out sesh” four times in a row, I know it’s because you are in pain.

I know that casual sex with strangers heals you. And you deserve all the self-care in the world.

My friends tell me you’re just making up excuses to not commit to a relationship. But what do they know. I mean, they haven’t even heard that beautiful song you wrote for me.

Beautiful blond woman With hair that glistens in the morning sun You smile that dances with the stars at night My beautiful blond woman

I cried the first time I heard you sing it at the dive bar across the street. Even though I’m a brunette, I know those gut-wrenching lyrics came from your Bad Boy heart.

You are my forbidden fruit. Hard and poisonous on the outside, soft and fleshy on the inside. And one day, I can’t wait to fix you.

I mean, love you.
